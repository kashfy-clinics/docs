Record everything going out: rent, salaries, supplies, utilities, one-off purchases. Expenses feed the [P&L](/finance/pnl). Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance → Expenses**.

The tab shows **Total expenses** for the selected range above the list.

## Adding an expense

Tap **Add expense** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Category** | Required. Search, or type a new one and tap **Add** |
| **Subcategory** | Optional |
| **Description** | Optional details, such as vendor or invoice number |
| **Amount (EGP)** | Required, more than 0 |
| **Payment method** | Cash, card, InstaPay, transfer, or other |
| **Date** | Required |
| **Branch** | A branch, or all branches (multi-branch clinics) |
| **Recurrence** | One-time, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly |
| **Ends on** | Optional, for recurring expenses |

## Recurring expenses

Choose a recurrence other than **One-time** and Kashfy adds a new copy of the expense automatically on each due date, until the end date if you set one.

Tap **Manage Recurring** to see every recurring expense with its next due date and end date, and to edit one.

<Tip>
  Use recurring expenses for fixed amounts like rent or salaries. For bills that change, like electricity, add them one at a time with the real amount.
</Tip>

## Filtering

Filter the list by **category** or **branch**, together with the date range at the top.

## Editing and deleting

Tap the pencil icon on any expense to edit it, or the delete icon to remove it. For a recurring expense, choose:

- **This occurrence only**
- **Stop recurring (keep past)**
- **Delete everything**
