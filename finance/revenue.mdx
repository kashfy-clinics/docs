title: 'Revenue'
description: 'See every payment received across appointments, lab, and radiology'
---

The Revenue tab shows money coming in. It pulls from completed appointments, lab intakes, and radiology intakes across the selected date range.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance → Revenue**.

## Summary cards

Three tiles up top summarize the period:

- **Total revenue** - sum of every payment recorded in the range
- **Average per payment** - revenue divided by number of payments
- **Insurance coverage** - share of revenue paid via insurance vs. cash

## Appointment stats

Below the tiles, four counters show the appointment volume behind the money:

- **Total** - appointments in the range
- **Completed** - marked completed by staff
- **Cancelled** - cancelled by patient or clinic
- **Pending** - still reserved, not yet resolved

## Payments table

Every payment in the range appears in the table with:

- **Date** paid
- **Patient** name (click to open the patient's record)
- **Amount** in EGP
- **Method** - cash, card, InstaPay, or transfer
- **Insurance** - which insurer, if any
- **Notes** - free-text notes staff added at payment time

Filter and sort by any column. Click a row to see the underlying appointment or intake.

<Info>
  Payments are recorded automatically when staff mark an appointment or intake as paid. There's no separate "log a payment" step - payment lives on the appointment/intake itself.
</Info>
