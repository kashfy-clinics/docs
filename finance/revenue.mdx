The Revenue tab shows money coming in during the selected date range. Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance → Revenue**.

## Where the numbers come from

Revenue is built from the payments your team records on visits (see [Payments on the visit sheet](/appointments/managing-appointments#payments)). There's no separate step. Each payment counts on the day it was recorded, minus any discount. Voided payments are left out.

## Summary cards

- **Total revenue** - all payments in the range, with the number of payments
- **Average per payment**
- **Insurance coverage** - how much insurers covered, and how many payments were insured (shown when you have insured payments)

## Appointments

Four counts for bookings scheduled in the range:

| Count | Includes |
|-------|----------|
| **Total** | All bookings |
| **Completed** | Completed visits |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled and no-show bookings |
| **Pending** | Reserved and arrived, not finished yet |

## Payments table

Each payment shows the **date**, **patient**, **amount** (and any discount), **method** (cash, card, InstaPay, transfer, or other), **insurance** company, and **notes**.

<Tip>
  To correct a payment, open the visit and void it, then record it again. Only owners and admins can void.
</Tip>
