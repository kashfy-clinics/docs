## Reporting an outage

If you're experiencing an issue that looks like a system-wide problem (not specific to your clinic's configuration):

1. Email [support@kashfy.com](mailto:support@kashfy.com) with the subject line **Possible Outage**
2. Include your clinic name and a description of what's not working
3. The team will investigate and update this page during confirmed incidents
