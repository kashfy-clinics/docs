Tap any booking on the board or calendar to open its **visit sheet**. Everything about that visit happens here.

## What's on the visit sheet

| Section | What you do there | Who |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| **Patient name** | Tap **Patient record** to open the full [patient profile](/patients/patient-profile) | Everyone |
| **Date & time** | See when the visit is | Everyone |
| **Patient contact** | Call or message the patient | Everyone |
| **Symptoms** and **Notes** | What the patient or staff wrote when booking | Everyone |
| **Stage** | The current [status](/appointments/appointment-status) | Everyone |
| **Diagnosis** | Type and **Save** | Doctors, owner, admins |
| **Prescription, scans, lab tests** | See below | Doctors, owner, admins |
| **Doctor notes** | Chart notes with attachments | Everyone |
| **Payments** | Record and void payments | Everyone except doctors |

## Moving the visit along

The buttons at the bottom change with the stage:

| Button | Moves to |
|--------|----------|
| **Mark arrived** | Arrived |
| **Start exam** | In progress |
| **Mark completed** | Completed |
| **No-show** | No-show |
| **Cancel** | Cancelled |
| **Reopen** | Back to Reserved (cancelled or no-show bookings) |

You can also drag the card between columns on the board. See [Appointment Statuses](/appointments/appointment-status) for which moves are allowed.

<Tip>
  When reception marks an unpaid visit **Completed**, Kashfy asks for the payment right away, pre-filled with the doctor's visit price. Tap **Skip** to record it later.
</Tip>

## Prescription, scans, and lab tests

One editor holds all three and saves them together:

- **Prescription** - search a drug (or add it by name), then write instructions such as "1 tab every 8h after meals". **Preview**, **Print**, or **Send to patient** on WhatsApp. See [Prescriptions](/appointments/prescriptions).
- **Select Scans** - opens a searchable picker of scans grouped by category. Tick the scans and tap **Confirm**, then add the clinical indication.
- **Select Tests** - the same picker for lab tests, with notes for the lab.

Orders appear on the patient's profile under **Lab & radiology**, and results show there once a Kashfy lab or radiology centre delivers them. See [Lab](/lab/overview) and [Radiology](/radiology/overview).

## Doctor notes

Write a chart note and tap **Save**. Tap **Attach** to add photos or PDFs (up to 20 MB each). Notes stay on the patient, so you see earlier notes from past visits here too.

## Payments

Tap **Add** in the Payments section (or the **Add payment** button on the card):

- **Amount** and an optional **discount** with a reason
- **Payment method**: cash, card, InstaPay, transfer, or other
- **Covered by insurance**: choose the insurance company and the amount it covers. The sheet shows what the patient pays.

If the amount is above the doctor's price, Kashfy asks you to confirm with **Record anyway** (for extra services). Owners and admins can **Void** a payment with a reason. Payments feed [Finance](/finance/revenue).

<Warning>
  A paid visit can't be marked cancelled or no-show, or moved out of Completed. Void the payment first.
</Warning>

## Booking a follow-up

Tap **Book follow-up** at the bottom of the sheet. The new booking form opens for the same patient.

## Rescheduling

There's no reschedule button. Cancel the booking and create a new one, or use **Book follow-up**. The AI can also cancel a booking when the patient asks on WhatsApp.
