Doctors write prescriptions inside the appointment. Kashfy turns it into a branded PDF and can send it to the patient on WhatsApp.

## Open the prescription editor

In **Appointments**, click an appointment to open its details. **Prescription & orders** is in the **Visit** card, under the diagnosis. Doctors, owners and admins can edit it; other staff can view it.

It has three matching blocks: **Medicines**, **Lab tests** and **Radiology**, each with a count of what's added.

## Write the prescription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add medicines">
    Search for a drug by name and pick it from the list. If it isn't there, choose **Add "..." manually**. You can use the keyboard: **↑ / ↓** to move, **Enter** to add, **Esc** to close. A drug that's already added shows **Added** and isn't added twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add instructions">
    Under each medicine, write how to take it, or tap a shortcut such as **1 tab 3× daily after meals**, **Once daily**, **Before sleep**, **When needed**, **For 5 days** or **For 1 week**. Shortcuts add to what's already written.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add lab tests or scans (optional)">
    Tap **Add tests** or **Add scans** to open the picker, tick what you need, and add notes for the lab or the clinical indication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It saves by itself">
    Everything saves automatically a moment after you stop typing. You'll see **Saving...** and then **Saved** next to the title. Closing the visit sheet straight away still saves. Use **Preview** to see the PDF before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The PDF includes your clinic logo and name, the date, the doctor, the patient's name and age, the medicines with instructions, any lab and radiology requests, and your clinic addresses and phone. Medicines, lab tests and radiology each get their own page. Once created, **Open** and **Print** buttons appear next to the title.

## Handwrite or dictate it

Instead of adding drugs one by one, you can write the prescription by hand or say it out loud, and Kashfy's AI reads it for you. Both buttons, **Handwrite it** and **Dictate it**, sit above **Medicines** for doctors, owners and admins.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Handwrite it">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Write">
        Tap **Handwrite it** and write on the pad with a stylus or your finger, the way you would on paper: drugs, doses, tests and scans. **Undo** removes the last stroke and **Clear** starts over.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read it">
        Tap **Read it**. A pen traces over your writing while Kashfy reads it. This takes a few seconds.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dictate it">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Record">
        Tap **Dictate it**, then tap the microphone and say the prescription. Egyptian Arabic with English drug names works, for example "Augmentin 1 gram, one tab every 12 hours for a week, and a CBC". Tap stop when you're done. A recording can be up to 1:30. The browser asks for microphone access the first time.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read it">
        Tap **Read it**, or **Record again** to start over.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Review before it's added

Nothing joins the prescription until you confirm. Kashfy shows what it read or heard, then everything it found:

- **Medicines** with their instructions. Each is marked **In list** when it matched a drug in the list, or **New name** when it didn't, so you can double-check it. You can edit the instructions here.
- **Lab tests** and **scans**, matched to the names in the pickers.

Untick anything you don't want and tap **Add ... to the prescription**. The items land in the blocks below and save automatically, like anything you type. A drug that's already on the prescription isn't added twice; if it had no instructions, the new ones fill in. New drug names are saved to your clinic's drug list for next time. If the result isn't right, tap **Try again** to go back to your writing or recording.

<Tip>
  Instructions come back in your dashboard language: natural Egyptian Arabic when the dashboard is in Arabic, English when it's in English.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Kashfy doesn't keep the drawing or the recording. Only the items you add are saved. If you read many in a row, you may be asked to wait a minute before the next one.
</Note>

## Prescription code

Every prescription has a short code, printed as **كود الروشتة** (for example `RX-1A2B3C4D`). When a patient sends this prescription back on WhatsApp, for example to a Kashfy lab or radiology centre, the AI recognises it and uses what was prescribed. It only does this when the prescription belongs to the patient who sent it.

## Send it on WhatsApp

Click **Send to patient**. You'll see "Prescription sent to the patient on WhatsApp" when it's done. You can send lab tests or scans on their own, without any medicines.

| Patient's last message to Kashfy | Patient receives |
|----------------------------------|------------------|
| Within the last 23 hours | The PDF file directly in the chat |
| Longer ago, or never | An approved template message from the doctor with a button to download the prescription |

<Warning>
  The patient needs a valid Egyptian mobile number to receive the prescription. If there isn't one on file, the editor shows **Patient WhatsApp number required to send prescription** with a box to add it. The send button stays disabled until the number is saved and at least one medicine, test or scan is added.
</Warning>
