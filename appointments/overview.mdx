The Appointments section is your live view of everything booked at your clinic. All bookings — whether made via WhatsApp, the website widget, or manually by your staff — appear here.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Appointments**.

## Three views

Use the view switcher at the top to switch between:

| View | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Board** | Kanban columns by appointment status (default view) |
| **Calendar** | Week, month, or day calendar with time slots |
| **Waitlist** | Patients waiting for the next available slot |

## Board view (Kanban)

The board has five active columns representing where an appointment is in the visit lifecycle:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Reserved** | Appointment is booked and upcoming |
| **Arrived** | Patient is at the clinic, not yet seen |
| **In Progress** | Doctor is currently with the patient |
| **Completed** | Visit is done |
| **No-show** | Patient did not arrive |

Cancelled appointments are hidden by default. Use the **Cancelled** filter chip at the top to show them.

Each card on the board shows the patient's name, doctor, time, and any reported symptoms. Walk-in appointments show a queue number.

**Filters in board view:**

- **Date range** — defaults to today; use the date picker to jump to any day or period
- **Branch** — filter by location if you have multiple branches
- **Search** — find by patient name, doctor name, or symptoms

## Calendar view

Switch to **Calendar** to see appointments on a calendar grid. Choose between:

- **Week** — the default; shows all appointments across the week in time slots
- **Month** — full month overview
- **Day** — single day with hour-by-hour breakdown

Click any appointment block to open its details. Click an empty time slot to create a new appointment.

## Waitlist

The **Waitlist** holds patients who want to book but couldn't find a suitable slot. When a slot opens up, you can contact them directly and convert them to a confirmed booking.

Each waitlist entry shows the patient's name, phone, preferred doctor, preferred date (if given), and any notes.

**Actions per entry:**

- **Contact** — opens WhatsApp to message the patient
- **Call** — dials their phone number
- **Booked** — marks them as booked and removes from the list
- **Remove** — removes them from the waitlist without booking

To add someone to the waitlist: switch to **Waitlist** view and click **Add to list**.

## Creating an appointment manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the appointments page">
    Go to **Dashboard → Appointments** and click **New Appointment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the branch">
    If you have multiple locations, choose which branch the appointment is at.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the doctor">
    Choose which doctor the appointment is for. The system will load that doctor's available slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    Select an available date, then choose from the available time slots. Unavailable times are greyed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter patient details">
    Enter the patient's full name and phone number. Optionally add symptoms or a reason for the visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm booking**. The appointment is created and appears in the board immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Appointment channels

Appointments flow into Kashfy from three sources:

| Channel | How it works |
|---------|-------------|
| **WhatsApp** | Patient messages your clinic's WhatsApp, AI books the appointment automatically |
| **Widget** | Patient uses the chat widget on your website, AI books the appointment |
| **Manual** | Your staff creates the appointment directly from the dashboard |

## Appointment reminders

Kashfy automatically sends a WhatsApp reminder to the patient the day before their appointment. No action required from your staff.

<Info>
  Reminders are only sent for appointments booked via WhatsApp or the widget, where the patient's phone number is already linked to a WhatsApp account.
</Info>
