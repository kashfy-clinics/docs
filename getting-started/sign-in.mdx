Kashfy runs at [app.kashfy.site](https://app.kashfy.site). Owners, staff, and doctors all use the same sign-in page. Kashfy never sends you SMS codes.

## Creating an account

Choose **Sign up** and either:

- **Continue with Google**, or
- Enter your **full name**, **email**, and a **password** (at least 8 characters), then type the **8-digit code** Kashfy emails you.

You must agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy first. After confirming, you go straight to [onboarding](/quickstart#2-complete-onboarding).

<Info>
  Staff and doctors don't sign up this way. They join through an invite from the clinic. See [Accepting an invite](#accepting-an-invite) below.
</Info>

## Verifying your mobile number

Your mobile number is verified by **you sending us a code** on WhatsApp, not by us texting you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your number">
    Go to **Settings → Personal** (doctors: **Settings → Personal** in the Doctor Portal), tap **Add mobile number**, and enter an Egyptian mobile number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Verify on WhatsApp">
    Kashfy shows a short message with a 6-digit code, a button to open WhatsApp, and a QR code you can scan from your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message as it is">
    Send it from that same number to Kashfy's WhatsApp. The page confirms automatically within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Send the prefilled message without editing it. The code is valid for 15 minutes. A number can only be linked to one Kashfy account.
</Warning>

## Signing in

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Email and password** | Enter both and tap **Sign in** |
| **Google** | Tap **Continue with Google** |
| **WhatsApp** | Tap **Continue with WhatsApp** (see below) |

### Sign in with WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your mobile number">
    Tap **Continue with WhatsApp** and enter the number verified on your Kashfy account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the code">
    Send the prefilled sign-in message from that number to Kashfy's WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The page signs you in automatically on the same browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes. If no account uses that number, sign in with email first and verify the number in your personal settings.

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in page tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email, type the code Kashfy emails you, then choose a new password. You can also change your password while signed in from **Settings → Personal → Change Password** (a code is sent to your email).

## Accepting an invite

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="user-plus" title="Staff and admins (email invite)">
    - **New to Kashfy:** you get an invitation email. Open it to set up your account; you're already added to the clinic.
    - **Already have a Kashfy account:** the clinic sends you an invite link. Open it while signed in with the invited email and tap **Accept invite**. The link is valid for 7 days.
    - Creating an account from an invite link asks for your name, an email code, and a mobile number verified on WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="stethoscope" title="Doctors (invite link)">
    The clinic adds you on the Doctors page and sends you an invite link. Open it, continue with Google or verify your email with a code, then set a password and tap **Activate account**. If the clinic entered your email, only that email can accept the link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## More than one clinic

One account can belong to several organizations (for example, a doctor who works at two clinics). Use the organization switcher in the sidebar to change which one you're working in.
