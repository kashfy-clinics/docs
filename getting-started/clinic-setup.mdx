Most setup happens in onboarding (see [Quick Start](/quickstart)). This page is the checklist for everything after that.

## Required before the AI replies

Kashfy will not let the AI answer patients, and your public page stays offline, until these are filled in under **Settings → Clinic**:

| Item | Where |
|------|-------|
| Arabic name | Clinic Info |
| Public URL | Clinic Info |
| Logo | Brand |
| Description | Clinic Info |
| Phone number | Contact |
| At least one address | Addresses |

Owners and admins see a yellow banner at the top of the dashboard that lists what is missing, with an **Open settings** button.

<Info>
  While required items are missing, patients who message you get a polite reply instead of the AI. You can still try the AI in **Dashboard → AI Agent**.
</Info>

## Recommended next steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add doctors and their hours">
    Every booking needs a doctor with hours at a branch. See [Doctors](/doctors/overview) and [Schedules](/doctors/schedules).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your services">
    List the procedures you offer with their prices so the AI can answer. See [Services](/services/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your insurance companies">
    Pick the insurers you accept and their coverage. See [Organization Settings](/settings/organization#insurance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on reminders">
    Set how many hours before a visit patients get a WhatsApp reminder. See [Reminders](/appointments/reminders).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your mobile number">
    Needed for signing in with WhatsApp. See [Sign-up & Sign-in](/getting-started/sign-in#verifying-your-mobile-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Reception staff and admins by email, doctors by invite link. See [Team Members](/team/members).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Branches

Each branch has its own address, area, and Google Maps link, and lists what it offers (clinic, lab, radiology). Doctors choose which branches they work at, and staff can be limited to specific branches. Manage branches in [Organization Settings](/settings/organization#addresses).
