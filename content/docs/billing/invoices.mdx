Invoices are listed under **Dashboard → Settings → Billing**. Your latest one is also on the [Subscription](/settings/subscription) page.

## When invoices are issued

| Plan | Invoice |
|------|---------|
| **Monthly** | On the 1st of each month, for the previous calendar month. If your plan started mid-month, the first invoice charges only the days it was active. |
| **Yearly** | Once a year, after your plan year ends. The plan year starts on your activation date. |

Invoices are only issued for active plans. Nothing is invoiced during the free trial.

## What's on an invoice

- **Plan fee**
- **Bookings** counted in the period, and how many your plan includes
- **Extra bookings** times your plan's extra-booking price
- **Total** in EGP
- **Due date** and a **reference** like `KSH-1A2B3C4D`

See [what counts as a booking](/billing/plans#what-counts-as-a-booking).

## Invoice statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Awaiting payment** | Issued, not yet paid |
| **Overdue** | Past the due date and still unpaid |
| **Paid** | Payment confirmed by the Kashfy team |
| **Void** | Cancelled by the Kashfy team |

## Overdue invoices

You can still pay on the due date itself. If the invoice is unpaid the day after:

1. The invoice becomes **Overdue**.
2. Your account is **suspended**: the AI stops replying to patients and new bookings can't be made, from WhatsApp, the widget, your public page, or the dashboard.
3. A red banner on the dashboard links to the invoice.

Your data stays available while suspended. Service comes back once the Kashfy team confirms your payment and marks the invoice paid.

<Warning>
  Before the due date, a blue banner reminds you of the amount and due date. Pay early to avoid any pause.
</Warning>

## Questions about an invoice

Contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site) with the invoice reference.
