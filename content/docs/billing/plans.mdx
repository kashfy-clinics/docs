Kashfy charges per booking, not per doctor or per message. Every plan includes every feature: the AI receptionist on WhatsApp, the knowledge base, reminders, the clinic, lab and radiology modules, and the full dashboard. Plans differ only in included bookings, number of doctors, the website widget, the "Powered by Kashfy" badge, and support priority.

<Info>
  The live price list is always at [kashfy.site/en/pricing](https://www.kashfy.site/en/pricing). This page matches it as of October 2026.
</Info>

## Free trial

Your **first 30 bookings are free**. No card and no time limit.

- From 20 bookings, a banner shows how many free bookings are left.
- At 30, the AI assistant pauses and the dashboard asks you to choose a plan.
- The first time a patient messages you after that, Kashfy opens a **one-time grace period**: the AI keeps working for **7 days or 20 more bookings**, whichever comes first.
- After the grace period the AI stays paused until a plan is activated.

<Info>
  The free trial is once per owner. If your verified mobile number already started a trial for another organization, a new organization starts with the trial and grace period already used.
</Info>

## What counts as a booking

A booking is counted once, the first time the visit actually happens. It doesn't matter whether the AI, the widget, or your staff created it.

| Event | Counted? |
|-------|----------|
| Appointment reserved | Not yet |
| Patient marked **Arrived**, **In progress**, or **Completed** | Yes |
| Appointment cancelled or no-show (never arrived) | No |
| Lab or radiology result delivered | Yes |

Once a booking is counted it stays counted, even if its status changes later.

## Extra bookings

Bookings never stop when you pass your plan, and neither does the AI. Bookings above your plan's included amount are charged at your plan's extra-booking price on the next invoice. The [Subscription](/settings/subscription) page shows your projected total as you go.

## AI fair use

AI messages have a fair-use allowance that grows with your bookings, set well above normal use. If a clinic goes well past it, the Kashfy team contacts you to find a better-fitting plan. **Passing it never pauses or switches off the AI.**

## Activating or changing a plan

There is no online payment. To activate a plan:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Open **Settings → Subscription → View plans** (or follow the **Choose a plan** banner) and tap **Contact us to activate** on the plan you want. The Kashfy team gets your request right away and contacts you. The button changes to **Request sent, we'll contact you**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the plan fee">
    By InstaPay, mobile wallet, or bank transfer. See [Payments](/billing/payments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the receipt">
    Send the transfer receipt to the Kashfy team on WhatsApp. They activate your plan, usually the same day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To switch plans later, contact the Kashfy team the same way.
