Every booking belongs to a doctor, and the AI only offers times inside a doctor's hours. Navigate to **Dashboard → Doctors**.

Owners and admins add and edit doctors. Employees can view the list. The counter at the top shows how many active doctors you have out of your plan's limit (1 on Solo, 5 on Team, unlimited on Clinic).

## Adding a doctor

Tap **Add doctor**. The form has four parts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic info">
    - **Photo** (optional) and the **Active** switch
    - **Name** in Arabic and English
    - **Specialty** (required) - search the list
    - **Bio** in Arabic and English (required). The AI uses it when patients ask about the doctor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pricing">
    - **Visit price** in EGP (required). The AI quotes it, and it pre-fills the payment.
    - **Follow-up price** (optional)
    - **Recheck window** in days (default 7). After a booking, the AI offers a follow-up this many days later.
    - **Doctor's email** (optional, new doctors only). If set, only this email can accept the invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Availability">
    Choose the doctor's branches (at least one), then set the weekly hours and booking mode for each branch. See [Doctor Schedules](/doctors/schedules).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Time off">
    Add days off or one-off hours. See [Time Off](/doctors/availability).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tap **Save**. You need at least one branch in [settings](/settings/organization#addresses) before adding a doctor.

## Inviting the doctor

When you save a new doctor, Kashfy shows a **Doctor invite link**. Copy it and send it to the doctor (for example on WhatsApp). Doctors who haven't joined yet show **Not joined yet**, with a **Copy invite link** button if you need the link again.

The doctor opens the link, signs in with Google or an email code, sets a password, and lands in the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal).

## Editing a doctor

Tap the doctor (or **Edit** in the row menu). Changes apply right away, including what the AI tells patients. Switch between **Table** and **Cards** at the top of the list.

## Deactivating a doctor

Turn off the **Active** switch. Inactive doctors aren't offered to patients and don't count toward your plan's doctor limit. Their history stays.

## Deleting a doctor

Choose **Delete** from the row menu. If the doctor has past visits, Kashfy deactivates them instead of deleting, so patient records stay complete.
