The Dashboard is the first page of the doctor view (**Dashboard** in the sidebar, `/doctor/dashboard`). It shows your own day and your own patients, not the whole clinic.

At the top, a greeting with today's date and two shortcuts: **Today's appointments** and **Ask the assistant**.

## Numbers

| Tile | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Today** | Today's bookings, and how many are still to see |
| **Next 7 days** | Open bookings in the coming week |
| **Your patients** | All your patients, and how many new ones this month compared with last month |
| **Visits this month** | Visits so far this month, compared with last month |
| **No-show rate (90 days)** | The share of bookings in the last 90 days where the patient didn't come, and this month's no-shows |

## Today

**Up next today** lists the patients still to come, each with their queue number or time, and **First visit** for new patients. Tap a patient to open their record, or **All appointments** to go to your appointments.

## Needs attention

| Row | Counts |
|-----|--------|
| **Follow-ups due** | Patients whose follow-up date has passed |
| **Doses or rechecks due today** | Injections, doses or rechecks scheduled for today |
| **Results still pending (30 days)** | Tests and scans ordered in the last 30 days with no result yet |
| **Files to check** | Patient files from the last 7 days waiting for your review |

Under **Most overdue**, the patients with the longest-overdue follow-ups, with how many days late. Tap one to open their record.

## Trends

- **Visits, last 14 days**: a bar chart of visits per day.
- **Busiest hours (90 days)**: when your patients usually come.
- **Top diagnoses (90 days)**: your most common diagnoses and their share.
- **New vs returning (90 days)**: first visits compared with returning patients.

## Recent artifacts

Your latest [artifacts](/doctors/artifacts), each with a preview. Tap one to open it in the Assistant, or **See all** for the Artifacts page.
