Doctors get their own workspace, separate from the clinic dashboard, that shows only their own work. They sign in at [app.kashfy.site](https://app.kashfy.site) like everyone else and land in the portal.

## Joining

The clinic adds the doctor on the Doctors page and sends them an invite link. The doctor opens it, continues with Google or verifies their email with a code, sets a password, and taps **Activate account**. See [Inviting the doctor](/doctors/overview#inviting-the-doctor).

  <Accordion icon="calendar-days" title="Appointments">

See [Managing Appointments](/appointments/managing-appointments).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="users" title="My Patients">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="messages" title="Conversations">
    The WhatsApp and widget chats of the doctor's patients, useful context before a visit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="robot" title="Assistant">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="receipt" title="Procedures">

## My schedule

Doctors manage their own hours from **My schedule** in the sidebar. Every change applies to booking right away, and the clinic's owners, admins and reception get a notification.

### Weekly hours

Under **Your weekly hours**, turn on the days you work and set a start and end time for each. If you work at more than one branch, pick the branch first: each branch has its own hours, and two branches can't overlap on the same day. Choose **Time slots** or **Walk-in queue** (with a daily patient limit), then tap **Save hours**. Session length is set by the clinic.

### Days off, breaks and different hours

Under **Upcoming days off and breaks**:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Day off** | Closes a whole day, or a range of days (up to 60 at a time). |
| **Break** | Blocks a time range on one date. The rest of that day stays bookable. |
| **Different hours for a day** | Replaces your usual hours for one date only. |

Each one can apply to every branch or to one branch, with an optional reason. Remove an entry from the list to make that time bookable again.

### Patients who are already booked

If a change leaves booked patients outside your hours (a day off, a break, different hours, or new weekly hours), Kashfy lists them and asks you to confirm. When you tap **Confirm and notify them**:

- Each patient gets one message, on WhatsApp from the clinic's number or by SMS when WhatsApp isn't possible, with a link to book another time.
- Reception is notified with the number of patients affected, and who couldn't be messaged.
- Nothing is cancelled. Reception moves the bookings.

Owners and admins still manage every doctor's schedule from **Dashboard → Doctors**. See [Doctor Schedules](/doctors/schedules) and [Time Off](/doctors/availability).

## What doctors can't do

- See other doctors' bookings or patients
- Create bookings (reception does this)
- See or record payments (dentists can see their patients' treatment plan balances, but not record money)
- Change clinic settings, AI settings, billing, or the team

## Settings

Under **Settings** in the portal:

| Tab | What's there |
|-----|--------------|
| **Personal** | Photo, mobile number ([verified on WhatsApp](/getting-started/sign-in#verifying-your-mobile-number)), name in Arabic and English, specialty, bio, and change password |
| **Public Page** | The doctor's public profile page (below) |
| **Prescription** | The doctor's own prescription paper design. See [Prescription Design](/settings/prescription) |
| **My prices** | Dentists only: their prices for dental procedures. See [Dental Prices](/dental/prices) |

## Public doctor page

Each doctor can have a public page at `doctors.kashfy.site/ar/<username>` to share with patients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Enable public page">
    When it's on, anyone with the link can view the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a username">
    7 to 40 characters: lowercase English letters, numbers, and hyphens. Kashfy tells you if it's available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand it">
    Upload a logo (or **Use my profile photo**) and pick a brand color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show WhatsApp number (optional)">
    Shows the doctor's verified mobile number on the page. Add the number in **Personal** first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page shows the doctor's photo, name, specialty, bio, the clinics they work at (with a **View clinic** link), and patient ratings. Copy or open the link from the same tab.

## Owners and admins who are doctors

If you're the clinic's owner or an admin and also one of its doctors, you get both views:

- You land on the **doctor view** (this portal) by default.
- Tap **Clinic management** in the sidebar to switch to the full clinic dashboard, and **Doctor view** to come back.

Kashfy remembers the view you picked last and opens it next time. For you, the [Assistant](/doctors/assistant) covers the whole clinic's patients, not only your own.

## Doctors at several clinics

A doctor can belong to more than one clinic with one account. A switcher in the sidebar changes which clinic the portal shows.

## Branch restrictions

If the clinic limited the doctor to certain branches, the portal only shows bookings and patients at those branches.
