The [Doctor Assistant](/doctors/assistant) tries to be helpful, and sometimes that means it gives an answer that is wrong or misleading. That's why the line under the message box always starts with **Kashfy AI can make mistakes**.

## Why it happens

The Assistant runs on a large language model. Models like this can produce text that reads well and sounds sure of itself but is not true. This is often called **hallucination**, and it's a known limit of today's AI, not something specific to Kashfy.

In practice, it can:

- **Misread your data.** It looks answers up in Kashfy, but it can pick the wrong patient with a similar name, the wrong visit, or the wrong date.
- **Fill gaps with guesses.** When a record is missing something, it may guess instead of saying it doesn't know.
- **Sort a paper wrongly.** An uploaded lab or scan paper can get the wrong type, title or date.
- **Get medical facts wrong or out of date.** Its general medical knowledge comes from training, can be old, and can be wrong even when it sounds confident.
- **Misquote.** A number, dose or quote can look exact and still be made up.

## How to use it safely

- **Don't treat it as the only source.** Check anything important against the patient's record, the original paper, or a trusted reference.
- **You make the clinical decisions.** The Assistant is not a doctor. Diagnoses, prescriptions and doses are your call.
- **Open the source.** When an answer points to a patient, a visit or a file, open it and check the details yourself.
- **Read before you confirm.** Nothing is saved to a patient until you tap to confirm. Check the patient, type and date first.
- **Review artifacts before you share them.** Read every [artifact](/doctors/artifacts) before you print it or send it to a patient.

<Warning>
  Never rely on the Assistant alone for anything that affects a patient's care, such as a dose, an allergy or a lab value. Always check the record.
</Warning>

## Tell us when it's wrong

- Tap the **thumbs down** under a reply that was wrong or unhelpful. It helps us improve the Assistant.
- For anything else, write to [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site).
