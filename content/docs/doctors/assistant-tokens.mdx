Every answer from the [Doctor Assistant](/doctors/assistant), every paper it sorts and every [artifact](/doctors/artifacts) it writes uses AI **tokens**. Tokens are counted per doctor.

## Your monthly allowance

- Each doctor gets **500K tokens every month**, included in the subscription.
- The allowance renews on the **1st of each month** (Cairo time). Unused tokens don't carry over.

## Seeing what's left

The line under the message box shows your balance, for example **Kashfy AI can make mistakes. 429K tokens remaining**.

Point at it, or tap it, to see:

- your monthly allowance (plus any packs bought this month),
- how much is left and the date it renews,
- a **Learn more** link to this page.

A tap keeps it open until you tap anywhere else.

## What uses tokens

| Action | Uses tokens |
|--------|-------------|
| Each question and answer | Yes, longer conversations use more |
| Sorting an uploaded lab or scan paper | Yes |
| Writing or editing an artifact | Yes, a full dashboard or explainer uses more than a short letter |
| Opening, sharing or printing an artifact | No |

## Buying more

When your tokens run out, you can't send new messages and papers are filed unsorted. The line under the message box turns into a **Buy more tokens** button. Tap it and pick a pack:

| Pack | Price |
|------|-------|
| 100K tokens | EGP 50 |
| 200K tokens | EGP 100 |
| 500K tokens | EGP 250 |

The tokens are added instantly and last until the end of the month. The pack is billed on the clinic's next [invoice](/billing/invoices).
