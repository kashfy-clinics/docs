title: 'Doctor Assistant'

The Assistant is an AI colleague for doctors. Ask it about your patients and your day in plain words, and it looks the answer up in Kashfy. Attach a photo or PDF of a lab result or scan, and it files the paper to the right patient once you confirm.

## Who can use it

The Assistant is for **doctors only**:

- **Doctors** see only their own patients, the same as in **My Patients**.
- **Owners and admins who are also one of the clinic's doctors** can use it too, and see the whole clinic.

Reception staff (members) can't use it.

You'll find it in two places:

| Where | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Assistant** in the doctor view's sidebar (`/doctor/assistant`) | The full page, with your conversation history. |
| **Assistant** button on the visit page | Opens a side panel about that patient. It reopens your latest conversation about them, and a paper with no printed name is filed to them by default. Tap **New chat** (+) to start over. |

## Conversations

The full page keeps every conversation in a history list on the side.

- **New chat** starts an empty conversation.
- **Search…** filters conversations by title. Press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on a Mac) to jump to it.
- Hover a conversation (or tap it on a phone) to **Rename** (pencil) or **Delete** (trash) it. Deleting asks first. Files already saved to patients stay on their records.

On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top to open the history.

## Linking a patient

Tap **+ Link a patient** beside the paperclip and search by **Patient name or phone** (2 letters or more). Once linked:

- The Assistant talks about that patient, so "she" or "the patient" means them.
- Papers with no name printed on them are filed to that patient.

Tap the patient's name to change it, or the x to unlink.

## What you can ask

Ask in Arabic or English, for example: **Who do I have today?**, **Who's my next patient?**, **Which of my patients has allergies?**

| Ask about | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Patients** | Find a patient by name or phone, their allergies, chronic conditions and facts |
| **Visits** | The last visit, the most recent diagnosis, complaints, plans, procedures and drugs |
| **Files** | A patient's test results, scans, prescriptions and uploads |
| **Schedule** | Today's appointments or any other day |
| **Payments** | What a patient paid, discounts, and what they still owe, visit by visit |

While it works you see what it's doing (for example **Finding the patient** or **Checking the schedule**). You can tap the stop button to cancel an answer.

<Info>
  The Assistant only reports what is in the record. It never gives a new diagnosis or treatment advice, and it can't change anything: it only reads.
</Info>

## Artifacts

Ask for a letter, a dashboard, a chart, a table or an explainer, and the Assistant writes it as an **artifact** that opens beside the chat, ready to print or share with your team or the patient. See [Artifacts](/doctors/artifacts).

## Rich cards

Instead of long text, answers come with cards:

| Card | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Patient** | Name, age, phone, alerts and facts |
| **Search results** | Matching patients, each opening the patient's record |
| **Visits** | The patient's visits with complaint, diagnosis, plan, procedures and drugs, plus **Open the full record** |
| **Schedule** | The day's appointments with time, status, and **First** for first visits |
| **Files** | The patient's files, each opening the file |
| **Payments** | **Total paid**, **Discounts**, **Outstanding**, and each visit's **Bill**, **Paid** and **Left** |

## Uploading lab and scan papers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach the paper">
    Tap the paperclip (**Attach an image or PDF**). You can attach JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC or PDF files, up to 15 MB each and up to 5 per message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kashfy reads and sorts it">
    While it shows **Reading the paper…**, Kashfy sorts the paper as **Lab result**, **Scan** or **File** (anything else), gives it a short title such as "CBC" or "MRI right knee", and reads the patient name and date printed on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    You can add a question or send the paper alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the patient">
    The Assistant asks **Does this paper belong to:** and lists the best matches for the printed name (or the linked patient when there is no name). Tap **Yes, save** next to the right patient. If it's none of them, tap **Someone else** and search, or tap **Don't save** to discard the paper.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The saved paper appears in the patient's files with its title and type, and the chat shows **Saved to [patient]'s record**.

<Warning>
  Nothing is saved to a patient until you tap to confirm. The Assistant never reads, describes or interprets the results on the paper: it only sorts it and matches it to a patient.
</Warning>

## Tokens

Every answer, and sorting every paper, uses AI tokens.

- Each doctor gets **500K tokens per month**. The allowance resets on the **1st of each month** (Cairo time). Unused tokens don't carry over.
- The line under the input shows what's left, for example **Kashfy AI can make mistakes. 420K tokens remaining**. Point at it, or tap it, to see your monthly allowance, what's left and when it renews. See [Assistant Tokens](/doctors/assistant-tokens).

### Buying more tokens

When your tokens run out, you can't send new messages and papers are filed unsorted. The line under the input turns into a **Buy more tokens** button. Tap it and pick a pack:

| Pack | Price |
|------|-------|
| 100K tokens | EGP 50 |
| 200K tokens | EGP 100 |
| 500K tokens | EGP 250 |

The tokens are added instantly and last until the end of the month. The pack is billed on the clinic's next [invoice](/billing/invoices).
