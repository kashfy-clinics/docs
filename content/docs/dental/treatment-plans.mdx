---
title: 'Treatment Plans'
description: 'Plan procedures on each tooth with their prices, mark them done, and send the plan to the patient on WhatsApp'
---

A treatment plan is the work the dentist intends to do, each line with its price. Each patient has one active plan that carries across visits until it's finished.

## Building the plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Treatment plan">
    At the top of the chart, choose **Treatment plan**. Planned work shows dashed in orange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a procedure">
    The procedure buttons show their price. Composite filling is selected to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the tooth">
    The procedure is added to the plan at its price. Fillings and sealants are set per surface: tap each surface in the circle, and the price goes up for each extra surface. Tapping the same tooth again with the same procedure takes it off the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whole-mouth work">
    Exam, scaling and whitening aren't tied to a tooth. Add them with the **+** buttons above the plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't plan work on a missing tooth, apart from an implant or a bridge.

Prices come from your [price list](/dental/prices). The plan shows whether it uses the clinic's prices or the dentist's own.

## Marking work done

Tap **Mark done** on a line when the work is finished. Kashfy then:

- updates the chart, for example a root canal adds the canals and a crown covers the tooth
- adds it to the tooth's history, linked to today's visit
- takes the materials it uses out of [inventory](/dental/inventory)

Done lines can't be changed back, because the chart and the stock have already moved. To remove a line that isn't done yet, tap **×** and confirm.

<Tip>
  Crown, bridge and implant lines have a lab button. Use it to send the work to a dental lab with the tooth and procedure filled in. See [Dental Lab](/dental/dental-lab).
</Tip>

## Totals

Under the plan you'll see the **plan total**, how much is **done so far**, and the **balance due**. Payments and installments are in the [patient account](/dental/patient-account).

## Sending the plan on WhatsApp

Tap **Send plan on WhatsApp**. Kashfy shows the exact message first: every line with its price, the total, what's paid and what's left. You can switch it between Arabic and English. Tap **Send** to send it from your clinic's WhatsApp number. It's saved in the patient's conversation.

<Note>
  WhatsApp only accepts this message within 23 hours of the patient's last message to your clinic. Outside that window, Kashfy tells you it can't send yet.
</Note>
