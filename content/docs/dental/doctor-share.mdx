---
title: 'Doctor Share'
description: "Each doctor's fees for their work, what's paid out and what's still owed, month by month, now on the Payroll page"
---

Doctor share is now part of [Payroll](/team/payroll), for every specialty, not only dentists. The old **Doctor share** page opens **Payroll → Doctor share**.

- **Set each doctor's fees** on **Payroll → Pay and fees**: a fixed amount, an amount per visit or procedure, a percentage of the money collected or the work billed (with [dental lab](/dental/dental-lab) costs and materials taken off first if you want), tiers, and a monthly minimum. The two percentages the old page had were kept as percentage fees. See [Doctor fees](/team/payroll#doctor-fees).
- **See and pay the month** on **Payroll → Doctor share**: each doctor's numbers, fee lines, total, paid out and owed, with **Pay out**. A payout is recorded as a **Doctor shares** expense in [Finance](/finance/expenses), and can't be more than what's owed. See [Doctor share](/team/payroll#doctor-share).
- **Doctors** still see their own month under **My share** in the doctor portal.

Payroll is on the Team plan and above, and only the owner sees it unless they give **See and pay salaries** to a role.
