The **Clinic Agent** page is where you shape the AI that answers your patients: who it is, how it talks, what it must and mustn't do, and what it knows. Open **Clinic Agent** in the sidebar (`/dashboard/ai`). Only owners and admins see this page.

Changes apply from the patient's next message. Tap **Save settings** at the bottom; the button appears as soon as you change something.

## Persona

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Greeting message** | The first thing the agent sends any new patient: it introduces itself as Kashfy and asks for consent to record their data, with **I agree** and **No thanks** buttons (**موافق** and **مش موافق** in Arabic). It is fixed and can't be changed. After the patient agrees, the agent asks their name. |
| **Agent name** | Always **Kashfy** (كشفي in Arabic) and can't be changed: the law requires telling patients that Kashfy handles their data. |
| **Dialect** | **Egyptian** (the default) or **Modern Standard** Arabic (simple فصحى). Patients who write in English are always answered in English. |
| **Tone** | **Friendly** (warm and short), **Professional** (clear and to the point) or **Formal** (polished and respectful, no emoji). |

Each dialect and tone shows a sample reply so you can pick the one that fits your clinic.

<Info>
  If you chose the **In-chat booking page** in [WhatsApp Settings](/settings/whatsapp#how-patients-book), a patient who opens with a greeting or "I want to book" gets your greeting message followed by the booking button.
</Info>

## Knowledge the agent uses

The card shows how many **active doctors**, **active services** and **knowledge base entries** the agent answers from. Tap a row to manage it. The agent only quotes prices, doctors and services that are in your data.

## Test the agent

**Test as a patient** opens a test chat (`/dashboard/ai/new`) where you write as if you were a patient. It uses your real settings and data, but nothing is booked and no real patient is messaged. The **Training** tab in the same chat lets you add rules by chatting. See [Custom Instructions](/ai-agent/custom-instructions).

## Instructions

Collapsed by default. Tap the header to open it.

| Field | Use it for |
|-------|------------|
| **Your clinic's instructions** | Policies, offers, payment methods, anything the agent should know or do, written like a brief to a new receptionist |
| **When to hand off to a human** | Situations where the agent stops and passes the chat to your team (for example a complaint, a refund request or an emergency) |
| **Forbidden for the agent** | Things it must never do or say (for example offering discounts that don't exist) |

Each field holds up to 4,000 characters. The agent follows them as long as they don't conflict with your clinic's data or its safety rules: it never invents prices and never gives medical advice.

## Knowledge base

Collapsed by default. Add text notes or upload files with information the agent needs. See [Knowledge Base](/ai-agent/knowledge-base).

## Agent performance

The last 30 days:

| Number | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Agent replies** | Messages the agent sent to patients |
| **Avg. response time** | From the patient's message to the agent's reply |
| **Conversations handled** | Patient chats the agent replied in |
| **Handoff rate** | Share of those chats handed to your team |
| **Bookings by the agent** | Bookings the agent made |
| **Booking rate** | Bookings divided by conversations |

A chart shows the agent's replies per day.
