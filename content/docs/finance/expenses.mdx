Record everything going out: rent, salaries, supplies, utilities, one-off purchases. Expenses feed the [Income statement](/finance/pnl) and [Cash flow](/finance/cash-flow). Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance → Expenses**.

The tab shows **Total expenses** for the selected range above the list.

## Adding an expense

Tap **Add expense** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Category** | Required. Search, or type a new one and tap **Add** |
| **Subcategory** | Optional |
| **Description** | Optional details, such as vendor or invoice number |
| **Amount (EGP)** | Required, more than 0 |
| **Payment method** | Cash, card, InstaPay, transfer, or other |
| **Date** | Required |
| **Branch** | A branch, or all branches (multi-branch clinics) |
| **Recurrence** | One-time, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly |
| **Ends on** | Optional, for recurring expenses |

## Recurring expenses

Choose a recurrence other than **One-time** and Docistra adds a new copy of the expense automatically on each due date, until the end date if you set one.

Tap **Manage Recurring** to see every recurring expense with its next due date and end date, and to edit one.

<Tip>
  Use recurring expenses for fixed amounts like rent or salaries. For bills that change, like electricity, add them one at a time with the real amount.
</Tip>

## Filtering

Filter the list by **category** or **branch**, together with the date range at the top.

## Editing and deleting

Tap the pencil icon on any expense to edit it, or the delete icon to remove it. For a recurring expense, choose:

- **This occurrence only**
- **Stop recurring (keep past)**
- **Delete everything**

Adding, editing and deleting expenses needs the **Add and edit expenses** permission ([Roles & Permissions](/team/roles#permissions)). Seeing the tab needs **See Finance**. A member limited to some branches sees only those branches' expenses (not the ones for all branches), and can't record one for another branch.

## Automatic expenses [#automatic]

Docistra adds some expenses by itself: dental lab orders (**Dental labs**), stock received (**Supplies**), doctor share payouts (**Doctor shares**), and paid [payroll](/team/payroll#run) (**Salaries** and **Doctor shares**).

## Salaries [#salaries]

Above the list, the owner sees the switch **Show salaries to everyone who sees Finance**. It's off by default.

- **Off**: **Salaries** expenses show only to the owner and whoever has **See and pay salaries**. Others don't see those rows or the Salaries category, and the [Income statement](/finance/pnl) adds them to **Other running costs**, so the totals stay right.
- **On**: everyone who sees Finance sees the salaries.

<Tip>
  With a small team, a salaries total tells everyone what each person earns. Keep it off unless your finance people need the detail. You can also answer this in [Clinic decisions](/settings/decisions).
</Tip>
