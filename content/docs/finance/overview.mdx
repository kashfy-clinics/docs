Finance is your clinic's books: money in, money out, and what's left. Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance**.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Revenue" icon="arrow-trend-up" href="/finance/revenue">
    Payments recorded on visits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expenses" icon="receipt" href="/finance/expenses">
    Spending, including recurring bills.
  </Card>
  <Card title="P&amp;L" icon="calculator" href="/finance/pnl">
    Net profit and where the money goes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Date range

The date picker at the top applies to all three tabs. It starts on the **last 30 days**; pick any range you like.

## Who sees Finance

Only the **owner** and **admins**. Employees and doctors don't see it in the menu. See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles).

<Info>
  Labs and radiology centres have their own Finance page inside their section. See [Lab & radiology finance](/lab/finance).
</Info>
