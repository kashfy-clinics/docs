## Date range

The date picker at the top applies to every tab. It starts on the **last 30 days**; pick any range you like.

## Who sees Finance

The **owner** and **managers**, and anyone whose role has the **See Finance** permission (the ready-made **Finance manager** role has it). Everyone else doesn't see it in the menu. See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles).

The **Income statement** and **Cash flow** tabs are on the Team plan and above; the other tabs are on every plan. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans).

<Info>
  Labs and radiology centres have their own Finance page inside their section. See [Lab & radiology finance](/lab/finance).
</Info>
