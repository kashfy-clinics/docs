title: 'Profit &amp; Loss'
description: 'Net profit and an expense breakdown for any date range'
---

The P&L tab puts revenue and expenses side by side to answer "did we make money?" and "where does it go?". Navigate to **Dashboard → Finance → P&L**.

## Summary

Three numbers for the selected range:

- **Total revenue** - payments recorded, minus discounts
- **Total expenses**
- **Net profit** - revenue minus expenses

## Expense breakdown

A bar for each expense category with its share of total spending, so you can see what's eating into your margin.

<Tip>
  If one category looks too high, go to [Expenses](/finance/expenses) and filter by that category to see the entries behind it.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Revenue counts when a payment is recorded, and expenses on the date you enter, so the P&L follows money as it actually moves.
</Info>
