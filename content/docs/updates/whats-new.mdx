## November 5, 2026

### Clinic decisions

A new **Settings → Decisions** page (Clinic decisions), for the owner. Kashfy asks you about your clinic's rules one at a time: attendance, deductions, leave, who runs salaries, the cash count, the biggest discount reception may give, and who sees what. Not sure? Press **I don't know, what do you recommend?**, or think it through in the **Think it through with Kashfy** chat. Each answer goes straight into the settings. New clinics land here right after sign-up. See [Clinic Decisions](/settings/decisions).

### Payroll, attendance and finance, refined

- Payroll, team attendance, and the income statement and cash flow are on the **Team plan and above** (and open during the free trial). See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans).
- **Mark as paid** now records two expenses: **Salaries** and **Doctor shares**. Doctor share payouts can't go past what's owed, overpaid amounts carry to next month, and people who join or leave mid-month are paid for their days. Tier bands follow on with no gaps. See [Payroll](/team/payroll).
- Leave counts working days, a forgotten check-out counts 0 hours until fixed, a double QR scan is ignored, and every change to the sheet is in a **Changes** log with **Restore**. See [Attendance & Leave](/team/attendance).
- Record what insurers pay on the **Cash flow** tab: it lowers **Owed by insurers**. The cash count is blind (reception's day summary hides the totals), finance managers get the switch and the alerts, and **End yesterday** closes a day left open past midnight. See [Cash Flow](/finance/cash-flow).
- The income statement compares with the previous calendar month, spreads ad spend by days, and follows the branch picker. The owner decides whether salaries show to everyone who sees Finance. See [Income Statement](/finance/pnl) and [Expenses](/finance/expenses#salaries).
- The money and salaries permissions are the owner's: only the owner gives them, edits them or moves someone into or out of a role that has them. Doctors choose whether their assistant works on their patients only or every patient in the clinic. See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles#sensitive) and [Doctor's Assistants](/team/assistants#which-patients).

### Cost and sell price when receiving stock

**Receive stock** now asks **Cost per unit (EGP)** and **Sell price per unit (EGP)**, for new and existing items. Managers see the profit per unit and if all are sold; the profit is counted when items sell, under **Finance → Stock profit**. See [Dental Inventory](/dental/inventory#receive-prices).

## November 4, 2026

### Payroll, doctor fees and doctor's assistants

A new **Payroll** page has every employee and doctor. Build each doctor's fees from a fixed amount, an amount per visit or procedure, a percentage, and tiers (for example 1 to 100 visits → X, 101 and above → Y, each band at its own rate or all at the band reached), with an optional minimum. The month's payroll adds them to salaries. **Doctor's assistant** and **Finance manager** are now built-in roles, and **Staff** is now called **Reception**. Each doctor decides on **My assistants** whether their assistant sees the full record or takes payments. See [Payroll](/team/payroll) and [Doctor's Assistants](/team/assistants).

### Team attendance, leave and salaries

A new **Attendance** page. You decide whether the team records attendance and how they check in: a button in the app, a code at reception that changes every 30 seconds, or their location at the branch. Set each person's hours, see the monthly sheet, and let the team ask for leave. Then decide whether attendance changes pay: deduct absences, unpaid leave and lateness automatically, or keep it as a record only. Pay the month in a few steps; it's recorded as Salaries and Doctor shares expenses. See [Attendance & Leave](/team/attendance) and [Salaries](/team/payroll).

### Income statement

The P&L tab is now a full **Income statement**: revenue by source, direct costs, gross profit, running costs (ad spend included) and net profit, each line compared with the period before, plus revenue by doctor and by branch, and a CSV download. See [Income Statement](/finance/pnl).

### Cash flow and the cash count

A new **Cash flow** tab shows the money that actually came in and went out, by payment method and day by day. Turn on **Cash count when ending the day** and reception counts the drawer when ending the day; Kashfy compares it with what should be there and tells whoever sees Finance when it's short. See [Cash Flow](/finance/cash-flow).

### Your own roles

**Settings → Team** has a new **Roles** tab. Edit the built-in roles, use the ready-made **Finance manager**, or make your own role and pick exactly what it can do: book, check in, take payments, see Finance, use the stock and more. Change anyone's role from the Team tab. See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles).

## October 29, 2026

### Operations and their profit

Surgeons who operate at outside hospitals can record each operation on the patient's file with its income and every cost, and see the net profit. Book the post-op review from the operation, and see every operation in **Finance → Operations**. See [Operations](/patients/operations).

### Referrals and session packages

Refer a patient to another specialty in your clinic from the visit and follow them until they start. Sell session packages, count sessions visit by visit, and see conversion on the new **Referrals** page. See [Referrals & Session Packages](/patients/referrals-packages).

### Clinic reports

A new **Clinic reports** tab in Analytics: waiting time, schedule use, attendance and revenue, by branch and provider, with CSV, Excel and PDF export. See [Clinic Reports](/reports/clinic-reports).

### Marketing

Record your ad spend in **Finance → Marketing** and see what each new patient cost and paid back, per source. See [Marketing](/finance/marketing).

### Booking from ads and the public page

- **Booking link for ads**: a tagged link per ad and campaign, so bookings show where they came from. See [Booking link for ads](/appointments/booking-link#ads-link).
- Patients can pick a service when booking on your public page. See [Choosing a service](/appointments/booking-link#service-choice) and [Services](/services/overview#bookable-online).
- **New patient** and **Returning** badges on bookings. See [New patient or returning](/appointments/booking-link#new-returning).

### Follow-up reminders

Kashfy reminds patients on WhatsApp a few days before a follow-up the doctor set, and offers to book. See [Follow-up reminders](/appointments/reminders#follow-up-reminders).

### Satisfaction survey

Send a short star-rating page after the visit instead of a chat question, and see satisfaction by provider and branch. See [Satisfaction survey](/reviews/overview#satisfaction-survey).

### Patient nationality

Record the patient's nationality from **Edit details**. See [Nationality](/patients/patient-profile#nationality).

## October 2026

### Clinic Agent settings

**AI Agent** is now **Clinic Agent**, with its own settings page: the agent's name, greeting, dialect (Egyptian or Modern Standard) and tone, your instructions, when to hand off to a human, what it must never do, the knowledge base, and its performance over the last 30 days. See [Agent Settings](/ai-agent/agent-settings).

### Artifacts

Ask the Doctor Assistant for a referral letter, a dashboard, a chart, a table or an explainer, and it writes it as an artifact beside the chat. Share it with your clinic team or specific teammates (view only), or send it to the patient as a link on WhatsApp. Every artifact lives on the new **Artifacts** page. See [Artifacts](/doctors/artifacts).

### Doctor Dashboard

The doctor view now opens on a dashboard: today's patients, follow-ups and pending results that need attention, visit trends, top diagnoses and recent artifacts. See [Doctor Dashboard](/doctors/dashboard).

### Keyboard shortcuts

Search, switch pages, start a new chat, attach files and more from the keyboard. Press **?** to see them all. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/getting-started/keyboard-shortcuts).

### Dental tools

Clinics with a dentist now get a full set of dental tools. See [Dental Overview](/dental/overview).

- **Tooth chart**: every tooth with its roots and five surfaces, with a history per tooth
- **Treatment plans**: tap a tooth to add a procedure at its price, mark work done, and send the plan on WhatsApp
- **Patient account**: payments on the whole plan, with monthly installments
- **Dental lab**: track work sent to labs, from sent to fitted
- **Dental inventory**: low-stock alerts, with materials taken out automatically when work is done
- **Doctor share**: each doctor's monthly percentage and payouts

### Private notes

Write staff-only notes on a patient from the chat or the patient profile. Only the author, the owner and admins see them; patients never do. See [Private Notes](/patients/private-notes).

### Faster prescriptions

- Prescriptions, lab tests and scans save automatically, with a **Saved** indicator
- One-tap instruction shortcuts and keyboard search for medicines
- Send lab tests or scans to the patient without any medicines
- Every prescription has a code (**كود الروشتة**) the AI recognises when the patient sends it back

### Clearer visit sheet and booking cards

The visit sheet keeps the patient and the next-step button in view, with a stage tracker and grouped sections. The diagnosis saves by itself. Booking cards show follow-ups, late patients, and which visits already have orders.

### Free trial and plans

- The free trial is now 30 bookings
- **Contact us to activate** now sends your request straight to the Kashfy team

## August 2026

### Patient Records Portal

Patients can now request a secure link to their appointment history directly through WhatsApp. The AI responds with a personal portal link at `patients.kashfy.site/ar/[patient-id]` - protected by a 4-digit PIN the patient sets on the spot.

- Patient asks for records ("ممكن ملفي" / "my records") and receives a secure link
- Patient sets a 4-digit PIN via WhatsApp on first request
- Link can be shared with any doctor; the doctor enters the PIN to access the records
- Portal shows all appointments with dates, doctors, diagnoses, prescriptions, and symptoms
- `?pin=XXXX` URL parameter auto-verifies on load for one-tap sharing
- Forgot PIN - patient sends "نسيت الكود" and sets a new 4 digits immediately
- Brute-force protection: 5 wrong attempts trigger a 30-minute lockout
- Sessions last 24 hours in the same browser after a correct PIN

### Doctor Portal - multi-clinic support

Doctors who consult at more than one clinic registered on Kashfy now see a clinic switcher in their sidebar. Switching clinics updates all sections - appointments, patients, and conversations - to reflect the selected clinic instantly. The previous behavior showed data from whichever clinic happened to load first, which was incorrect for multi-clinic doctors.
