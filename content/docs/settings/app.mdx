---
title: 'Install the App'
description: 'Install Kashfy on your phone or computer so it opens like a normal app'
---

Kashfy can be installed on your device so it opens in its own window, without a browser tab or address bar.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → App**. If your browser supports it, an **Install app** button appears at the top. Otherwise follow the steps below.

## Computer (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

1. Look for the **install icon** on the right side of the address bar.
2. Click it and choose **Install**.
3. Kashfy opens in its own window from now on.

<Note>
  Firefox and Safari on desktop don't support installing web apps. Use Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
</Note>

## Mobile

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="apple" title="iPhone (Safari)">
    1. Open Kashfy in **Safari**.
    2. Tap the **Share** button.
    3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="android" title="Android (Chrome)">
    1. Open the browser **menu** (three dots).
    2. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
