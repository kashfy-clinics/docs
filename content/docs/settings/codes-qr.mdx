All Kashfy centers share one Kashfy WhatsApp number. The codes on this card tell Kashfy which center a patient is trying to reach, and, for labs and radiology, which branch they just walked into.

Go to **Settings → Organization** and scroll to **Codes & QR**.

## Your organization code

Every organization has a short code. When a patient messages the Kashfy WhatsApp number with `كود` followed by your code (for example `كود A7X2K`), they go straight to your assistant instead of being asked which center they want.

The card gives you:

- **The code**, with a copy button
- **A WhatsApp link** that opens a chat with the message already filled in. Copy it for your Facebook page, Instagram bio, or website.
- **A QR** of that link, and **Print** for a ready poster titled "Message us on WhatsApp" with your name and code

<Tip>
  Put this QR on flyers, your door, or your social media. Anyone who scans it lands in a chat with your assistant.
</Tip>

If the card says **No code yet. Contact Kashfy support.**, reach out to [support](/support).

<Info>
  On your own WhatsApp number (see [WhatsApp Settings](/settings/whatsapp)), the same link and printed QR open a chat with your number directly, so posters you already printed keep working.
</Info>

## Branch check-in QR (lab and radiology)

This section appears if your organization has the Lab or Radiology type. Each active branch gets one QR per service (**Lab** and/or **Radiology**). The codes are created automatically when a branch offers that service, so you don't need to request them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the branch">
    Click a branch to show its QR codes. With more than 6 branches, use **Search branches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print">
    Click **Print** under the Lab or Radiology QR. You get a poster titled "Scan to check in" with the branch name and service. You can also copy the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it at reception">
    Patients scan it, send the WhatsApp message, and appear on that branch's [intake queue](/lab/intake).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Each QR belongs to one branch and one service. Put the lab QR at the lab counter and the radiology QR at the radiology counter, or patients will land on the wrong board.
</Warning>

See [QR check-in](/lab/qr-intake) for what the patient sees and how scans are matched to AI bookings.

## Organization code vs. check-in QR

| | Organization code | Branch check-in QR |
|--|-------------------|--------------------|
| **Used for** | Starting a WhatsApp chat with your assistant | Checking in at the counter |
| **Where to put it** | Anywhere: social media, flyers, website | At the reception desk of that branch |
| **What happens** | The patient talks to your AI assistant | The patient's card appears in **Arrived** on that branch's board |
| **Available for** | Every organization | Lab and radiology branches |
