Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Subscription**. Tap **View plans** at the top to compare plans and ask for activation.

## Free trial

While you're on the trial, a card shows how many of your **30 free bookings** you've used and how many are left. The bar turns amber at 20 and red at 30. See [how the trial works](/billing/plans#free-trial).

## Your plan

Shows the plan name, price per month or year, included bookings, doctor limit, and the price per extra booking. A plan you picked in onboarding shows **Chosen, not active yet** until the Kashfy team activates it. Once active it shows **Current plan** and the activation date.

## Usage this period

Active plans only. Shows bookings counted so far against your plan's included bookings:

| Usage | Bar color |
|-------|-----------|
| Under 80% | Green |
| 80 to 99% | Amber |
| 100% or more | Red |

Below the bar: the plan fee, any extra bookings times the overage price, and the **projected total**. Monthly plans count the current calendar month and are invoiced on the 1st for the previous month. Yearly plans count the plan year and are invoiced once, after the plan year ends.

See [what counts as a booking](/billing/plans#what-counts-as-a-booking).

## AI messages

Shows the AI's replies to patients this month against your **fair-use** allowance for the month. The allowance grows with your bookings: if you book more than your plan includes, it rises with them.

<Info>
  The AI never stops when you pass fair use. From 80% you'll see an amber note. Past 100%, the AI keeps running and the Kashfy team may contact you about a better-fitting plan. Only the Kashfy team can pause a clinic's AI; if that happens, this card says so and patients are told the clinic team will reply.
</Info>

## Latest invoice

Your most recent invoice with its period, reference (for example `KSH-1A2B3C4D`), status, and amount. Unpaid invoices show [payment instructions](/billing/payments). Tap **All invoices** for the full history.

## Cancel subscription

Owners on an active plan can tap **Request cancellation**. The Kashfy team contacts you to settle the last invoice and stop the subscription. You can **Withdraw request** until then.
