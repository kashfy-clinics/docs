Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Clinic**. The menu label matches your setup: **Clinic**, **Lab**, **Radiology Centre**, or **Organization** if you run more than one type.

Owners and admins can edit this page. Other roles can view it. Each section opens on its own and has its own **Save** button.

<Warning>
  The AI only replies to patients once the Arabic name, public URL, logo, description, phone number, and at least one address are set. See [Clinic Setup](/getting-started/clinic-setup).
</Warning>

## Organisation Types

Shows **Clinic**, **Lab**, and **Radiology Centre**, with an **Active** badge on the ones you have. See [Modules](/settings/modules).

## Insurance

Choose the insurance companies you accept and set a **coverage percentage** for each. Search the list, tap a company to select it, then save. These companies are offered when staff record an insured payment on a visit.

## Brand

- **Logo** - upload your logo. It appears on your public page.
- **Primary color** - the accent color for your public page.

## Clinic Info

- **Name** in Arabic and English
- **Public URL** - the address of your public clinic page. Tap the open icon to view it.
- **Description** in Arabic and English. The AI uses it to describe your clinic.

## Contact

- **Phone** - shared with patients who ask for a number
- **Public email**
- **Website**

## Patient Reminders

Turn on automatic WhatsApp reminders and set how many hours before each visit they go out (1 to 168 hours). See [Reminders](/appointments/reminders).

## Addresses

Each address is a branch. For each one set:

- **Area** - search the list or add a new area
- **Branch label** (optional), for example "Nasr City"
- **Street address**
- **Google Maps link** - open the branch on Google Maps, tap **Share**, and copy the link

Tap **Add address** for another branch. You must keep at least one.

## Codes & QR

- **Your WhatsApp code** - patients who send this code to Kashfy's WhatsApp reach your AI receptionist directly. Copy the code or link, or **Print** a QR poster.
- **Branch check-in QR** (labs and radiology only) - print it for the reception desk. The patient scans it, sends the WhatsApp message, and joins that branch's queue.

See [Codes & QR](/settings/codes-qr) for details.

<Tip>
  The **Copy ID** button next to the page title copies your organization ID, which Kashfy support may ask for.
</Tip>
