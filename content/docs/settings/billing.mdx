title: 'Billing'

---

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Billing**. Payment is by transfer. Pay each invoice by its due date to keep the AI assistant running.

## Each invoice shows

- **Period** covered and a **reference** such as `KSH-1A2B3C4D`
- **Amount** in EGP and **status**
- **Plan fee**
- **Bookings** counted, with how many your plan includes
- **Extra bookings** times the overage price, if any
- **Due date** (unpaid invoices) or **Paid on** date

Unpaid invoices include the [payment instructions](/billing/payments) right under them.

## Invoice statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Awaiting payment** | Issued and not yet paid |
| **Overdue** | Past its due date and still unpaid |
| **Paid** | The Kashfy team confirmed your payment |
| **Void** | Cancelled by the Kashfy team |

<Warning>
  If an invoice is still unpaid the day after its due date, your account is suspended: the AI stops replying and new bookings stop. See [Invoices](/billing/invoices#overdue-invoices).
</Warning>
