The **Waiting screen** turns any TV in your waiting room into a live board: for each doctor it shows who is with the doctor now and who is next. It updates by itself from your bookings and chimes when a patient is called. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Waiting screen**.

<Info>
  Available on every plan. Owners and managers set it up, in clinics.
</Info>

## Make a screen [#make]

1. Press **Make a screen**. Each TV gets its own screen, so press **Another screen** for every extra TV.
2. Press **Copy link** and open the link in the TV's browser. An Android TV box, or a computer plugged into a screen, works too. **Open** opens it on the device you're using.
3. Tap the screen once. Browsers only play sound after a tap, so until you tap, the bottom of the screen says **Tap the screen to turn the sound on**. The same tap makes it full screen, and the screen keeps the TV awake.

The TV needs no sign-in: the link is its key. Press the bin button to delete a screen, and its link stops working on the TV straight away. Under each screen you can see when the TV last connected.

<Warning>
  Keep the link to yourself. Anyone who has it can see the board.
</Warning>

## Screen settings [#settings]

Every choice saves at once, and the TV picks it up within a few seconds.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Name | A name for you, for example "Ground floor TV" |
| **Branch** | Which branch the screen shows, or **All branches** (when you have more than one branch) |
| **Doctors** | Which doctors the screen shows, or **All doctors** |
| **Patient name on screen** | **Number only**, **First name** (the first name and the next name's first letter, the default) or **Full name** |
| **Screen language** | **العربية** or **English** |

<Note>
  A psychiatrist's patients always show as numbers only, whatever you pick.
</Note>

## What the screen shows [#board]

One card per doctor who has patients today:

- **With the doctor now**: the patient in the exam, with their queue number in large type. Without a queue number (bookings by time), the booking time shows instead.
- **Next**: up to four patients marked arrived, in line order, then how many more are waiting.

Patients who are booked but not marked arrived don't show. So mark patients **Arrived** when they come in (or let them check in on the clinic QR code). See [Managing Appointments](/appointments/managing-appointments#moving-the-visit-along).

## Calling a patient [#call]

When the doctor or reception presses **Start exam**, the screen chimes and that doctor's card flashes with the patient's number. If the patient didn't hear, open the booking and press **Call again** (it shows only for a visit with the doctor, and only when the clinic has a screen). The screen chimes again.

The first version plays a chime only. There is no spoken announcement yet.
