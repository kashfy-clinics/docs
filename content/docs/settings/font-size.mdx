---
title: 'Font size'
description: 'Make headings and text bigger or smaller, for you only'
---

If the text in Kashfy feels too small (or too big) on your screen, change it here. Only the text changes size: buttons, spacing and icons stay where they are.

Go to **Settings → Font size**. Doctors find it in their own settings too.

<Info>
  The size is saved to **your account**, not the clinic. Each member of the team picks their own, and yours follows you to any phone or computer you sign in on.
</Info>

## The two sliders

| Slider | What it changes |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Headings** | Page titles and card titles |
| **Body text** | Lists, tables, messages, fields and labels |

Each slider goes from **85%** to **130%**. The small mark under the slider is the normal size (100%).

## Preview at real size

While you drag a slider, the **Preview at real size** box below shows a slice of a real screen (a title, a patient card and a schedule table) at the new size. Nothing else on the page changes until you save, and a **Not saved yet** note reminds you.

Click **Save** to apply the size everywhere in the app. To go back, click **Reset to normal** and then **Save**.

<Tip>
  If one size is enough for you, move both sliders together. Raising only **Body text** is useful when titles already look large but tables are hard to read.
</Tip>
