Go to **Settings → Security**. Doctors find it in their own settings too. Everything here belongs to **your account**, not the clinic: each member of the team sets up their own.

## Two-step verification [#two-step-verification]

With two-step verification on, signing in needs two things: your usual method (Google, phone code, password or email link) **and** a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still can't get in without your phone.

To turn it on:

1. Install an authenticator app, such as **Google Authenticator** or **Microsoft Authenticator**.
2. Click **Turn on**. A QR code and a key appear.
3. Scan the QR code in the app (or type the key by hand).
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows, then click **Turn on**.

From then on, every new sign-in opens a **Two-step verification** page that asks for the app's current code. A device that was already signed in also asks for it once.

<Info>
  The code changes every 30 seconds. If it's rejected, wait for the next code and type that one.
</Info>

To stop using it, click **Turn off** and confirm.

<Warning>
  If you lose the phone with the authenticator app, you can't finish signing in. Contact Kashfy support to remove two-step verification from your account.
</Warning>

## Active devices [#active-devices]

This list shows every browser and phone that is signed in to your account right now: the browser and system (for example **Chrome on Windows**), its IP address, and when it was last active. The device you're using is marked **This device**.

- **Sign out** next to a device signs that device out. It has to sign in again within an hour at most.
- **Sign out all other devices** signs out everything except the device you're using.

If you see a device you don't recognize, sign it out, change your password, and turn on two-step verification.

## Recent sign-ins [#recent-sign-ins]

The last 10 times someone signed in to your account, with the date, the device and the IP address. It stays even after a device signs out, so you can check where your account was used.
