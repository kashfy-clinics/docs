Personal settings belong to you, not the clinic. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Personal**, or open **My Account** from the menu under your name.

## What you can update

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="image" title="Profile photo">
    Tap the avatar to upload a new photo, or **Remove photo**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user" title="Full name">
    Shown across the dashboard and in the team list. Tap **Save** after editing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="phone" title="Mobile number">
    Tap **Add mobile number** (or **Change**), enter an Egyptian mobile number, and tap **Verify on WhatsApp**. You send a prefilled code message from that number and it shows **Verified**. See [verifying your number](/getting-started/sign-in#verifying-your-mobile-number).

    A verified number lets you [sign in with WhatsApp](/getting-started/sign-in#sign-in-with-whatsapp). It is your personal number, not the clinic's public phone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="lock" title="Change password">
    Tap **Send verification code**, enter the code sent to your email, then set and confirm a new password (at least 8 characters).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Email address

Your email is shown but can't be changed here. Contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site) if you need to change it.

<Note>
  The demo account can't edit personal settings.
</Note>
