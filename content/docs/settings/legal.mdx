title: 'Legal Papers & the Verified Badge'
description: "Upload your clinic license and each doctor's Medical Syndicate ID so Docistra can verify them and show the Verified badge to patients"
---

Egyptian law requires every clinic to be licensed and every doctor to be licensed to practice. To keep patients safe, Docistra verifies both. Upload your clinic's legal documents and your doctors' Medical Syndicate IDs, and once we've checked them your clinic gets the **Verified** badge that patients trust. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Legal papers**.

<Info>
  Free on every plan. Owners and managers upload the papers, in clinics. Your clinic keeps working normally while the papers are missing or under review.
</Info>

## What to upload [#papers]

| Paper | Required? |
|---|---|
| **Clinic license**: the medical facility license from the private treatment department | Required (not for an independent doctor, who works at other clinics) |
| **Medical Syndicate ID** for every active doctor, both sides | Required for each doctor |
| **Commercial register** | Optional |
| **Tax card**, both sides | Optional |
| **Other papers** | Optional |

Files can be PDFs or photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC), up to 10 MB each. They are stored privately: only you and the Docistra review team can open them. Patients never see your papers, only the badge.

<Note>
  The doctor list comes from your active doctors. Add your doctors first (see [Doctors](/doctors/overview)), then upload each one's card.
</Note>

## Send them for review [#review]

1. Press **Upload** (ارفع) next to each paper and pick the file. Press **Add another** (ارفع تاني) to add a second file, for example the back side.
2. When every required paper is in, press **Send for review** (ابعت للمراجعة).
3. The status changes to **Under review**. We'll send you a notification when the review is done.

While the papers are under review they are locked. Message us on WhatsApp if you need to change something.

## After the review [#after]

- **Approved**: your clinic shows the **Verified** badge next to its name on its public page, and your doctors show it on their doctor pages.
- **Needs changes**: we'll tell you what to fix in a notification and on the page. Upload the corrected papers and press **Send for review** again.

When a new doctor joins after approval, the page asks for their Medical Syndicate ID. Upload it at any time: we check it on its own, and the clinic keeps its badge meanwhile.

## The daily reminder [#reminder]

Until your papers are in, owners and managers see a short popup once a day. **Upload papers** opens this page; **Remind me tomorrow** hides it until the next day. It also comes back when a new doctor still has no card.

## The Verified badge [#badge]

The blue check next to a name on a public page means Docistra checked the papers:

- On a **clinic page**: the clinic's license and its doctors' cards were approved.
- On a **doctor page**: that doctor's Medical Syndicate ID was accepted.
