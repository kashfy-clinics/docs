Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings**. It opens on **Personal**. On desktop the sections are in a side menu; on mobile they scroll across the top.

## Settings sections

| Section | Shown to | What it controls |
|---------|----------|------------------|
| [Personal](/settings/personal) | Everyone | Your name, photo, mobile number, and password |
| [Clinic / Organization](/settings/organization) | Everyone (owners and admins edit) | Profile, branding, contact, branches, insurance, reminders, [Codes & QR](/settings/codes-qr) |
| [WhatsApp](/settings/whatsapp) | Everyone (owners and admins edit) | Connect your clinic's own WhatsApp number (or one per branch), templates, usage, booking style, chat import |
| [Telegram](/settings/telegram) | Everyone (owners and admins edit) | Connect a Telegram bot (or one per branch) so the assistant answers and books there too |
| [Insurance & Partners](/settings/insurance) | Labs and radiology centres | Which insurers each lab or radiology branch accepts |
| [Prescription](/settings/prescription) | Clinics | The prescription paper design: one design for all doctors or one per doctor |
| [Dental & prices](/dental/prices) | Dental clinics | Your dental procedures and price list |
| [Widget](/settings/widget) | Clinics | Website chat widget |
| [Notifications](/settings/notifications) | Everyone | Alerts you receive |
| [App](/settings/app) | Everyone | Install Kashfy on your phone or computer |
| [Subscription](/settings/subscription) | Everyone | Free trial, plan, and usage |
| [Billing](/settings/billing) | Everyone | Invoices and how to pay them |
| [Team](/team/members) | Everyone (owners and admins invite) | Staff and admins |
| [Font size](/settings/font-size) | Everyone | Bigger or smaller headings and text, for you only |

<Info>
  The **Clinic** label changes with your setup: **Lab**, **Radiology Centre**, or **Organization** when you run more than one type. Enabling a lab or radiology centre is done from that page. See [Modules](/settings/modules).
</Info>

Doctors have their own settings in the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal#settings): **Personal** and **Public Page**.
