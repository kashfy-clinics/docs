The prescription design is the look of the prescription patients get as a PDF, on WhatsApp, and in print. Clone your printed paper from a photo, or design it from scratch, and watch a live preview as you go.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Prescription**. Doctors find it in the doctor view under **Settings → Prescription**.

## Who can edit

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner / Admin** | Choose **One design for all doctors** or **A design for each doctor**, and edit any of them |
| **Doctor** | Edit their own design |
| **Everyone else** | See the design (read only) |

### One design or one per doctor

Owners and admins pick one of two options at the top:

- **One design for all doctors**: a single shared paper. Each doctor's own name fills in wherever `{doctor}` is written.
- **A design for each doctor**: every doctor gets their own paper. Pick the doctor from **Choose a doctor** to edit theirs.

A doctor with no design of their own starts from the clinic's shared design. The page says **This is the clinic's shared design. Saving makes it this doctor's own.**

## Clone your paper from a photo

The quickest way to start is to copy the paper you already print.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take a photo of your printed prescription paper">
    A clear, straight photo works best. JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Upload a photo">
    Under **Clone your paper from a photo**. While it shows **Reading your paper…**, Kashfy reads the page size, the main color, the Arabic and English sides of the header, the logo position, the title and R/ mark, the fill-in fields and their labels, and the footer (addresses, phones, website, email, colored band and a faint background logo).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logo cut out">
    Kashfy cuts the logo out of the photo and uses it as the paper's logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, then tap Save design">
    Check every detail in the preview and fix anything it misread. Nothing is saved until you tap **Save design**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When you clone a shared clinic design, the doctor's name on the paper is replaced with `{doctor}` so it works for every doctor. For a doctor's own design, the name is kept as printed.
</Tip>

## Designing from scratch

Every part of the paper can be changed. The preview on the side updates as you type, using sample patient details.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="sliders" title="Page and color">
    Choose **A4** or **A5**, and pick one of the preset colors or a custom color. The color is used for headings, lines and the footer band.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user" title="Header">
    The header has an **Arabic side** (right) and an **English side** (left). Each side has a name line and up to 8 lines under it, such as your specialty and credentials.

    Write `{doctor}` for the name so each doctor's own name shows, for example `Dr. {doctor}` or `د. {doctor}`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="image" title="Logo">
    Place the logo **Left**, **Center** or **Right**, or choose **None**. Drag the slider to change its size, and tap **Replace logo** to upload a different image. Without one, the clinic logo is used.

    Turn on **Faint logo in the background** for a light copy of the logo behind the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="file-medical" title="Title and fields">
    Set the title above the patient details (for example **Prescription**) and turn the **R/** mark on or off.

    The fields are **Date**, **Patient Name**, **Age**, **Diagnosis**, **Doctor** and **Rx code**. Turn each one on or off, rename its label, and use the up and down arrows to reorder them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="building" title="Footer">
    Add your **Addresses (one per line)**, up to 6, and your **Booking numbers**, website and email. Turn on **Colored band** to put the footer on a band of the paper's color.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

When you're done, tap **Save design**. New prescriptions use it straight away. **Unsaved changes** shows while you have edits that aren't saved.

### The default design

If nothing has been designed yet, Kashfy starts from a default: A4, dark blue, `Dr. {doctor}` / `د. {doctor}` in the header, the logo in the center, the **Prescription** title with R/, the Date, Patient Name, Age, Diagnosis and Rx code fields, and a colored footer band with your branch addresses and clinic phone filled in.

## Reset to default

Tap **Reset to default** and confirm to remove the design you're editing. New prescriptions then use the next design in line (see below).

## Which design a prescription uses

When a prescription is made, Kashfy uses:

1. The visit doctor's own design, if they have one
2. Otherwise, the clinic's shared design
3. Otherwise, the default design

This is true whichever option the owner picked at the top: a doctor's own saved design always comes first.
