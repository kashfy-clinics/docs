Every appointment has one stage. Staff move it from the board or the visit sheet, and the AI cancels bookings when a patient asks on WhatsApp.

## Stages

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="clock" title="Reserved">
    Booked and upcoming. Every new booking starts here, whoever made it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user-check" title="Arrived">
    The patient is at the clinic, waiting to be seen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="stethoscope" title="In progress">
    The patient is with the doctor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-check" title="Completed">
    The visit is done. Reception is asked to record the payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user-xmark" title="No-show">
    The patient didn't come and didn't cancel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-xmark" title="Cancelled">
    Cancelled by the patient, your staff, or the AI. Hidden from the board unless you tap **Cancelled**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Allowed moves

| From | Can move to |
|------|-------------|
| **Reserved** | Arrived, In progress, Completed, No-show, Cancelled |
| **Arrived** | Reserved, In progress, Completed, No-show, Cancelled |
| **In progress** | Arrived, Completed, No-show, Cancelled |
| **Completed** | In progress only (to fix a mistake) |
| **No-show** | Reserved, Arrived |
| **Cancelled** | Reserved |

<Warning>
  - A **paid** visit can't be cancelled, marked no-show, or moved out of Completed. Void the payment first.
  - Bringing back a cancelled or no-show booking only works if its time slot is still free. If someone else took it, book a new time.
</Warning>

## Stages and billing

A booking counts toward your plan the first time it reaches **Arrived**, **In progress**, or **Completed**. Bookings that stay Reserved, or end Cancelled or No-show without arriving, are never counted. See [What counts as a booking](/billing/plans#what-counts-as-a-booking).

## Who can change stages

| Role | Can change |
|------|-----------|
| Owner, Admin | All appointments |
| Employee | All appointments, or only their branches if restricted |
| Doctor | Their own appointments only |
