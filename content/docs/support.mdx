## What to include in your support request

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include:

- Your **clinic name** and the email address used to sign up
- A **description of the issue** - what you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
- The **date and time** when the issue occurred
- Screenshots or screen recordings if relevant (especially for AI conversation issues)
- Your **WhatsApp conversation** or appointment ID if the issue is specific to a booking

## Common issues & quick fixes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="robot" title="AI stopped responding to patients">
    Kashfy runs on one shared, official WhatsApp number, so there is nothing to reconnect on your side. When the AI stops answering, it's usually one of these:

    - The AI is switched off for that patient in **Conversations**
    - Your subscription isn't active (check **Settings → Subscription**)
    - Your clinic profile is incomplete (name, public link, logo, description, phone and at least one address)

    See [When the AI stops answering](/ai-agent/overview) for the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="calendar-x" title="AI booked an incorrect time slot">
    Go to **Dashboard → Conversations** and find the conversation. Review what the patient asked and what the AI responded. If you find a recurring pattern, adjust the AI's custom instructions or doctor schedules to prevent it.

    To fix the incorrect booking, open the appointment and reschedule or cancel as needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="credit-card" title="Payment failed">
    Check with your bank that the card is enabled for online payments. Try again with the same card, or use a different card. If the problem persists, contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="eye-off" title="Widget not showing on website">
    Confirm the script tag is placed just before `</body>` in your HTML, that `data-clinic-id` is set to your actual Clinic ID, and that there are no JavaScript errors in your browser console.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Have an idea for a feature or an improvement? We'd love to hear it. Email [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site) with the subject line **Feature Request** and describe what you're looking for and why it would help your clinic.

Popular requests are tracked and prioritized on our [Roadmap](/updates/roadmap).
