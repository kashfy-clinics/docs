---

Reports are uploaded from the [intake queue](/radiology/intake) when you complete a card. The **Reports** page in the Radiology sidebar lists completed cases so you can check delivery and resend. Delivery works exactly like lab results; see [Results](/lab/results) for the full details.

## Uploading a report

Open a card that's **In progress**, click **Choose result file** (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC, up to 20 MB), check the details, and click **Upload & send**. The card moves to **Complete**.

## How the patient receives it

| Situation | What the patient gets |
|-----------|-----------------------|
| The patient messaged Kashfy on WhatsApp in the **last 23 hours** | The **report file itself** on WhatsApp |
| Otherwise | A WhatsApp message with a button to a **secure results page** with Open and Download buttons, valid for **30 days** |

Report files are private and only reachable from inside Kashfy or through the patient's secure link. If a [referring doctor](/lab/referrals) was added with consent, they get their own link too.

## The Reports page

Each completed case shows the patient, mobile, referring doctor, scans, completion time, and **Sent to patient** or **Not delivered**. Use **View report** to open the file and **Resend** to send it again (up to 3 times per card per hour).

<Info>
  If the patient had no mobile number, the case is completed but nothing is sent. Open the card on the intake queue, add the number with **Edit**, then resend.
</Info>

## Fixing a wrong report

Open the completed card on the intake queue, click **Back**, and upload the corrected file. It replaces the old one and is sent again.
