## What is Kashfy?

Kashfy is a clinic management system with an AI receptionist built in. Patients book on WhatsApp or your website in Egyptian Arabic, and your team runs the day from one dashboard: appointments, patient files, prescriptions, payments, and finance.

Kashfy also supports **labs** and **radiology centres**, on their own or alongside a clinic.

## How a booking works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Patient sends a message">
    The patient messages Kashfy's WhatsApp number with your clinic code, or uses the chat widget on your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The AI understands the request">
    It answers questions about doctors, prices, and location, and offers open slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booking confirmed">
    Once the patient confirms, the appointment appears on your board instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit day">
    Reception marks the patient arrived, the doctor writes notes and a prescription, and staff record the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who is Kashfy for?

Egyptian clinics, labs, and radiology centres that want to:

- **Stop missing patient messages** that arrive after hours
- **Reduce reception workload** by automating booking conversations
- **Talk to patients in their own dialect**, not formal Arabic
- **Keep every patient's history in one place**

## What Kashfy does not do

- Kashfy does **not collect money from patients** online. Your team records payments taken at the clinic.
- The AI handles **bookings and questions only**. It does not give medical advice.
