Moving from another clinic system? Bring your data into Kashfy, then review and import it from **Dashboard → Data migration**. Nothing is added to your clinic until you press **Import** yourself.

Only owners and managers can open **Data migration**.

## How it works [#how-it-works]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring the data in">
    Use the Chrome extension for a web-based system, or upload files (Excel, CSV, a database backup, or photos of paper records). The data waits in a holding area; nothing touches your patients or appointments yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Kashfy works out what each table is and which column goes where, and shows a preview: what will be added, what is already in Kashfy, and what would be skipped and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    If the preview looks right, press **Import** on its card. You can then ask the assistant to check the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 1. Bring your data in [#bring-data-in]

### From a web-based system: the Chrome extension [#extension]

The [Kashfy Chrome extension](/getting-started/chrome-extension) reads your old system for you. You show it a few screens once (about 3 minutes), it tries 3 patients so you can check them here, then it moves everything by itself: patients, doctors, visits, procedures, payments, files and your price list. It only reads, and never changes anything in the old system.

Press **Get the Chrome extension** on **Data migration**, then follow the [Chrome extension guide](/getting-started/chrome-extension).

### From a desktop program or paper: upload files [#upload-files]

Press **Upload files** and pick one or more files:

- **An export from your old program**: Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or a SQLite database backup. The file is read in your browser and only its rows are sent. Save old .xls and Access files as .xlsx or CSV first.
- **Photos or PDFs of paper records**: patient files, notebooks, forms or receipts, handwritten or printed, in any layout (up to 40 MB and 400 pages per file). On a phone the same button opens the camera. Kashfy reads each page and sorts what it finds into patients, visits, procedures and payments. A page about one patient is also saved in that patient's files.

<Tip>
  Handwriting can be misread, so check a few rows in the preview before you import. If a page can't be read, photograph it again in better light and upload it again.
</Tip>

### History kept outside the old system [#outside-history]

Procedures or payments written on paper, or typed into Excel, can be added after you import the old system. They are linked to patients by phone number. A procedure or payment on a day the patient has no visit gets a past visit on that day. If the clinic has several doctors, give the sheet a doctor column.

For dental clinics, a **Tooth** column (36, or 14, 15, 16) and a **Surfaces** column (MO, MOD) put the work on the patient's [tooth chart](/dental/tooth-chart), and an **Amount paid** column next to each procedure becomes a payment for that visit.

## 2. Review and import [#review-and-import]

Open **Dashboard → Data migration**. The **Summary** shows what arrived: pages sent, tables found, and a card per type with how many were found, added, already there, waiting, skipped or failed.

Talk to the assistant below, or tap a suggestion such as **شوف البيانات اللي وصلت وقولي فيها إيه** (Look at what arrived and tell me what's in it). It:

- shows how each table was sorted: which Kashfy field each column fills, with an example value (data the extension read usually arrives already sorted)
- leaves out rows that aren't clinic records (menus, headers, totals)
- previews each import: **Will be added**, **Already in Kashfy**, **Will be skipped** and **Will fail**, with reasons
- points out what looks missing

To import, press the button on a preview card. Import in this order: **doctors**, then **patients**, then **appointments**, then **procedures** and **payments**, then **files**, since each is linked to the ones before it. The assistant never imports anything by itself.

After an import, ask the assistant to check it (**اتأكد إن كل حاجة اتنقلت صح**). It compares what arrived with what is now in Kashfy and explains any difference.

If an import fails, nothing changes and you can try again.

## What is imported [#what-is-imported]

| Data | Details |
|------|---------|
| Patients | Name, phone, gender, date of birth or age, insurance, allergies, chronic conditions, current medications, medical history, referral source |
| Doctors | Name, specialty, consultation and follow-up prices |
| Visits | Date and time, patient, doctor, status, visit type, notes, diagnosis, complaint |
| Procedures | The procedures done in each visit |
| Payments | Patients' payments, linked to their visit |
| Files | Attachments, x-rays and documents, saved in the patient's files |
| Services | Your services and procedures list with the old prices. A procedure you already have with the same name gets the old price. |

Settings of the old system (payment methods and the like) are not moved.

### How duplicates are handled [#duplicates]

- **Patients** are matched by phone number, then by name. A patient already in Kashfy is not added again; only details missing from their record (such as gender or date of birth) are filled in.
- A new name on a phone number that already belongs to a patient is added as a family member of that patient, the same way [family accounts](/patients/patient-profile) work elsewhere in Kashfy.
- **Doctors** are matched by name.
- **Appointments** are matched by patient and start time.
- You can bring in more data and import again at any time: only what is new is added.

### Appointments [#appointments]

- **Past appointments** are imported as history: completed, cancelled or no-show. They don't count toward your plan's bookings or your trial, and patients get no messages about them.
- **Upcoming appointments** are imported as booked, and patients get your clinic's normal [reminder](/appointments/reminders) before the visit.
- An appointment whose patient isn't in Kashfy yet creates that patient.
- An appointment whose doctor can't be found isn't imported. Import your doctors first, or check the doctor's name.

### Doctors and your plan [#doctors-and-plan]

Doctors beyond your plan's limit are added as **inactive**. Activate them from [Doctors](/doctors/overview) after upgrading, or deactivate another doctor first.
