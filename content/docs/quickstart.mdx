This guide takes you from a new account to your first booking through Kashfy's AI receptionist.

## What you'll need

- An email address (or a Google account)
- An Egyptian mobile number you use on WhatsApp
- Your clinic's name, logo, phone number, and address (with a Google Maps link)
- Each doctor's specialty, visit price, and working hours

## 1. Create your account

Go to [app.kashfy.site](https://app.kashfy.site) and choose **Sign up**. You can continue with Google, or enter your name, email, and a password (at least 8 characters). Kashfy emails you an 8-digit code to confirm the address.

See [Sign-up & Sign-in](/getting-started/sign-in) for every way to get in, including signing in with WhatsApp.

## 2. Complete onboarding

After sign-up, a short wizard sets up your organization:

<Steps>
  <Step title="What do you run?">
    Pick **Clinic / Doctors**, **Laboratory**, **Radiology centre**, or any combination (a hospital can enable all three). For a clinic, choose **Personal clinic** (one doctor) or **Group clinic** (several doctors).
  </Step>
  <Step title="How many doctors?">
    Group clinics only. Kashfy uses the number to recommend a plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name">
    The name patients will see in chats and on your public page. You can change it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branches">
    Add each branch with its area, street address (at least 5 characters), a Google Maps link, and what it offers (clinic, lab, radiology).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose the plan you intend to use and tap **Start trial**. Nothing is charged: your first 30 bookings are free, with no card and no time limit. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Verify your mobile number

Go to **Settings → Personal**, add your mobile number, and tap **Verify on WhatsApp**. You send a short prefilled message from that number to Kashfy's WhatsApp, and the number is confirmed automatically. A verified number also lets you [sign in with WhatsApp](/getting-started/sign-in#sign-in-with-whatsapp).

## 4. Finish your clinic profile

Open **Settings → Clinic** (called **Organization** if you run more than one type). The AI will not reply to patients until these are filled in:

- Arabic name
- Public URL
- Logo
- Description
- Phone number
- At least one address

A yellow banner at the top of the dashboard lists anything still missing. See [Organization Settings](/settings/organization).

## 5. Add your doctors

Go to **Dashboard → Doctors → Add doctor**. Set the specialty, bio, visit price, branches, and weekly hours. Kashfy gives you an invite link to send to the doctor so they can use the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal). See [Doctors](/doctors/overview).

## 6. Let patients reach you on WhatsApp

All clinics share Kashfy's official WhatsApp number. Your clinic has its own short code, shown under **Settings → Clinic → Codes & QR**. Patients who send that code reach your AI receptionist directly. See [Codes & QR](/settings/codes-qr) and [WhatsApp Setup](/ai-agent/whatsapp-setup).

## 7. Test the AI

Go to **Dashboard → AI Agent** and use the test chat. Try messages like:

- "عايز أحجز مع الدكتور"
- "امتى المواعيد المتاحة؟"
- "كام سعر الكشف؟"
