---

The **Finance** page in the Lab or Radiology sidebar shows the money your completed cases brought in. It covers both lab and radiology if your organization runs both.

<Info>
  Finance is visible to owners and admins only.
</Info>

## How revenue is counted

- Only **completed** cards count (the result was uploaded).
- The amount is the card's **Total (EGP)**, as entered at intake or edited on the card.
- Each case is dated by **when it was completed**, so later edits don't move it to another day.

<Tip>
  Revenue is only as accurate as the totals on your cards. Confirm the total before you upload the result.
</Tip>

## Picking a period

Switch between **Today**, **Week** (the last 7 days), **Month** (this calendar month), and **All**.

## What you see

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Revenue** and **Orders** | Total revenue and number of completed cases in the period |
| **Lab** / **Radiology** | Revenue and order count for each service |
| **Top items by revenue** | Your 10 highest-earning tests and scans. When a card has several items, its total is split by each item's price. |
| **Revenue by branch** | Revenue and orders per branch. Cards with no branch show as **Unassigned**. |
| **Recent transactions** | The latest completed cases |
