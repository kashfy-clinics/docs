title: 'Referrals & Session Packages'
description: 'Refer a patient to another specialty in your clinic, follow them until they start, and sell and count packages of sessions'
---

When a doctor sends a patient to another specialty in the same clinic (for example orthopaedics to physiotherapy, or internal medicine to nutrition), Kashfy keeps track of the referral by itself: whether the patient booked, started and finished. Treatments given in several sessions can be sold as a **session package** and counted visit by visit.

## Refer a patient [#refer]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Refer on the visit">
    On the [visit page](/appointments/visit), tap **Refer** in the header. Doctors refer from their own visits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the referral">
    - **Specialty**: one of your clinic's other specialties.
    - **Doctor (optional)**: a specific doctor, or **Any doctor of the specialty**.
    - **Diagnosis**: filled in from the visit; change it if needed.
    - **Plan and instructions**: what you want done, for example "knee strengthening, no flexion past 90 degrees".
    - **Suggested sessions (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Refer">
    The referral is saved. Referrals already made from this visit are listed under **Referred from this visit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If your clinic has no other specialty yet, add that doctor on the Doctors page first.
</Info>

## Referral statuses [#statuses]

The status changes by itself as the patient moves along:

| Status | When |
|--------|------|
| **Not booked yet** | The referral was made, and the patient has no booking with that doctor or specialty yet |
| **Booked** | The patient has a booking with the doctor, or with any doctor of the specialty |
| **Started** | That visit was completed, or a session was counted on the referral's package |
| **Completed** | The referral's package is used up |
| **Declined** | Closed by hand because the patient declined |
| **Cancelled** | Closed by hand because the referral was withdrawn |

A cancelled booking puts the referral back to **Not booked yet**. Doctors and managers can close a referral by hand with **Close referral**: **Patient declined** (with an optional reason), **Withdrawn**, or **Done** once the patient has booked or started. **Reopen** brings a declined or withdrawn referral back.

## Session packages [#session-packages]

### Sell a package

Tap **New package** in the visit's **Session package** card, or on the patient file's **Care journey** card. Enter the service, **How many sessions?**, the **Package price (EGP)**, and, for reception and managers, what was **Paid now (optional)** and how. A package sold for a referral is linked to it.

### Count a session

On a visit with that doctor or specialty, tap **Count this session**. The closing sheet offers it too when you finish the visit. Each visit counts once. Tap the undo arrow to take a session off the count. When the last session is counted, the package completes by itself.

### Payment

The package price is the charge. Reception and managers tap **Record payment** to add a payment on the package; it counts in Finance like any other payment. The package shows **Not paid**, **Partly paid** or **Paid**, and what's left.

### Stop and resume

**Stop package** asks why the patient stopped: got better, no improvement, cost, no time, travel or distance, medical reason, went elsewhere, no reason given, or other, with an optional note. **Resume** brings the package back.

## Care journey on the patient file [#care-journey]

The **Care journey** card on the patient file's **Overview** tab shows:

- Each referral: who referred, to what specialty, its status, and links to the referring visit and to the booking or first session.
- Its packages, with sessions done and left.
- The pain score over sessions, for physiotherapy and pain-management visits.
- The next visit or follow-up.

Reception sees the packages and the next visit, but not the referrals.

## The Referrals page [#referrals-page]

Owners and admins open **Referrals** in the sidebar. Doctors have **My referrals**, with only the referrals they wrote or received.

- **Last 90 days**: how many were referred, booked, started and completed, and the **Conversion** rate (patients who started treatment in the other specialty out of all referred).
- **Referred, not in yet**: patients referred 3, 7, 14 or 30+ days ago who still haven't come, each with **Call**, **WhatsApp** and **Close**.
- **Packages (last 90 days)**: sold, active, completed and stopped, with **Why they stopped**.
