---
title: 'Operations'
description: "Record operations done at outside hospitals on the patient's file, with income, every cost and the net profit, and book the post-op review"
---

Surgeons who operate at outside hospitals can record each operation on the patient's file: what was done, where, what it brought in and what it cost. Kashfy works out the total cost and the net profit, and the clinic sees every operation together in **Finance → Operations**.

The **Operations** card sits on the **Overview** tab of the [patient profile](/patients/patient-profile).

## Add an operation [#add-operation]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Add operation">
    On the patient's **Operations** card, tap **Add operation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the operation">
    - **Type of operation** and **Hospital**. Types and hospitals you used before show as chips you can tap.
    - **Operation date**.
    - **Surgeon**. It starts on you if you're a doctor, or on the clinic's only surgeon. Owners and admins can pick another doctor when the clinic has several.
    - **Insurance case**, if an insurer pays for it.
    - **Payment**: **Received** or **Pending**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the money">
    **Total income**, then each cost: **Hospital fee**, **Instruments**, **Assistant fee**, **Anaesthesia**, **Nurse fee** and **Other cost** (with a line for what it was). **Total cost** and **Net profit** update as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Save">
    The operation appears on the card with its type, date, hospital and net profit. A net profit below zero shows in red.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the doctor already noted the operation in the surgery section of a visit, a new record starts from that procedure and day.

Tap an operation to see its cost lines, the surgeon, notes and its post-op reviews. **Edit** changes it, and **Delete** removes it after you confirm. Reviews already booked from a deleted operation stay booked.

## Schedule the post-op review [#post-op-review]

Open the operation and tap **Schedule post-op review**. The normal booking sheet opens. The visit is booked with the surgeon as a follow-up and linked to the operation, so it shows under **Post-op reviews** on that operation.

## Finance → Operations [#finance-tab]

Owners and admins see every operation in **Dashboard → Finance → Operations**. The tab shows when the clinic has a doctor who operates (surgery, orthopaedics, gynaecology, urology, andrology, ENT or ophthalmology) or already has operations recorded.

For the selected date range (by operation date):

- **Operations income** with the number of operations, **Total cost**, **Net profit**, and **Pending from insurance**.
- **Insurance** vs **Cash**: the count, income and net profit of each.
- A table of every operation: date, patient, surgeon, operation, hospital, income, cost and net profit, with **Insurance** and **Pending** chips. Filter by surgeon, hospital and case type (**All**, **Insurance** or **Cash**).
- **Where the cost went**: hospital fee, instruments, assistant, anaesthesia, nurse and other, added up.

**Download CSV** gets the same rows with every cost line.

<Info>
  Operation money is paid at the hospital, so it is not part of [Revenue](/finance/revenue) or [P&L](/finance/pnl).
</Info>

## Who can see operations [#who-can-see]

| Role | What they see |
|------|---------------|
| **Owner and admins** | Every operation on every patient, and the Finance tab |
| **Doctors** | The card when their specialty operates or they already operated on the patient, and only the operations they did |
| **Reception** | Nothing. The card and the numbers are hidden |
