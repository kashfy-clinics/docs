---
title: 'Patient Records Portal'
description: 'Patients get a PIN-protected link to their own visits, diagnoses, prescriptions, and files'
---

The Records Portal lets a patient see their own medical history, and share it with any doctor, through a personal link protected by a 4-digit PIN. No app or account is needed.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Patient asks on WhatsApp">
    The patient asks for their file, for example "ممكن ملفي". If the phone number has family members, the AI asks whose file they want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Patient sets a PIN">
    The first time, the AI asks the patient to choose a 4-digit PIN.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Patient gets the link">
    The AI sends a personal link on `patients.kashfy.site`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open and enter the PIN">
    Anyone with the link and the PIN can open the records in a browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the portal shows

| Section | Contents |
|---------|----------|
| **Patient info** | Name, gender, date of birth or age, insurance details |
| **Appointments** | Past and upcoming visits (not cancelled ones) with date, doctor, symptoms, diagnosis, and prescription, including the prescription PDF when there is one |
| **Files** | Documents and images saved to the patient's file |

The portal is read-only. Records are only changed by the clinic in the dashboard. Doctor notes and [private notes](/patients/private-notes) are never shown to the patient.

## Link with the PIN included

Adding `?pin=1234` to the link verifies automatically when it opens, then removes the PIN from the address bar:

```
https://patients.kashfy.site/ar/<patient-id>?pin=1234
```

<Info>
  The portal is available in Arabic (`/ar/`) and English (`/en/`). Links from WhatsApp open in Arabic.
</Info>

## Forgotten PIN

The patient sends something like "نسيت الكود" to the clinic on WhatsApp. The AI clears the old PIN and asks for a new 4 digits. Anyone already viewing with the old PIN is signed out.

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="lock" title="PINs aren't stored as plain text">
    Kashfy stores a one-way hash of the PIN, tied to that patient.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="clock" title="24-hour sessions">
    After a correct PIN, the browser stays signed in for 24 hours. Resetting the PIN ends all sessions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="shield" title="Lockout after wrong guesses">
    After 5 wrong PINs, the portal locks for 30 minutes. Repeated guessing from one device is also blocked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  The link itself doesn't change. To stop someone you shared it with, the patient resets their PIN.
</Warning>
