Open a profile from **Dashboard → Patients** (tap a patient, then **Profile**), or tap **Patient record** on any appointment sheet. Doctors open it from **My Patients** in the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal). When a doctor opens a booking from the board, the profile opens on the [visit page](/appointments/visit); tap **Full record** there to see everything below.

## Header

The patient's name, phone, and badges such as **Allergies** or **Family member**, with quick actions:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Call** | Dials the patient |
| **Message** | Opens the patient's chat |
| **Book** | Books a new appointment (not available to doctors) |
| **Edit** | Edits the patient's details |

Below it, three numbers at a glance: **Next visit**, **Last visit**, and **Attendance** (visits attended and no-shows). If the patient is on the waiting list, a notice says so.

## Medical alerts

**Allergies**, **chronic conditions**, and **current medications**. Doctors, owners, and admins tap **Edit** to update them (type and press Enter, or pick a suggestion like Penicillin or Diabetes). Anyone with allergies on file gets the **Allergies** badge in the header.

## Facts

**Facts** are the patient's lasting history that the doctor collects over time, such as "Diabetic since 2020" or "Appendectomy 2015". They stay on the record across every visit, and the card sits in the side column on every tab, as well as on the [visit page](/appointments/visit).

- **Add** - type the fact in the box and tap **Add** or press Enter. Each fact shows the date it was added and who added it. A fact added from a visit is linked to that visit.
- **Remove** - tap the cross next to a fact that turned out to be wrong. You can write a reason (for example "retest came back normal"), then tap **Remove**, or **Cancel** to keep it.
- **Removed** - removed facts aren't deleted. Tap **Removed (2)** at the top of the card to see them crossed out, with the date, who removed them, and the reason.
- **Restore** - tap the restore arrow on a removed fact to bring it back.

<Info>
  Doctors can add, remove, and restore facts. Owners and admins can too if they also have a doctor profile at the clinic. Everyone else sees facts read-only.
</Info>

## AI briefing

The **Quick briefing** card (on the Overview tab and on the visit page) writes a few short lines about the patient, so the doctor knows the essentials in seconds. Tap **Summarize**, and **Refresh** later to update it.

Kashfy reads the patient's whole record for it: every visit with all of the doctor's sections (complaint, examination, lab and radiology results, provisional diagnosis, management plan, procedures), prescriptions, lab and scan orders, visit notes, medical alerts, and Facts. The briefing leads with allergies, chronic conditions, and Facts, then how the problem has changed across visits, the latest plan and prescription, pending results, and no-shows when they matter. A removed fact is never presented as current.

The card shows when the briefing was made. It's AI-generated, so double-check it before relying on it.

<Note>
  A doctor's briefing is built from their own visits only, the same visits they can see on the profile.
</Note>

## Details

Date of birth and age, insurance company and ID, who referred them, the assigned doctor, whether they accept WhatsApp messages, and when they became a patient. Tap **Edit** to change the name, gender, date of birth, insurance, or referral.

## Family

Children or relatives seen on the same phone number. Tap **Add member** and enter a name, gender, and date of birth. Each member gets their own profile and visits; the account holder is marked **Account holder**.

## What doctors see

Doctors see the profile of any patient they've had a booking with, but only their own visits. They can update medical alerts and Facts and write clinical notes, and they don't see the Billing tab or the Book button.

## Patient's own access

Patients can view their own records, with a PIN, through the [Records Portal](/patients/records-portal).
