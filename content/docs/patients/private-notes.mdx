---
title: 'Private Notes'
description: 'Staff-only notes on a patient that are never sent to the patient or shown on their records'
---

Private notes are for things your team needs to remember about a patient but the patient should never see, for example "check for irritation next visit, may switch the cream".

## Who sees a private note

| Person | Can see it | Can edit or delete it |
|--------|------------|------------------------|
| The person who wrote it | Yes | Yes |
| Owner and admins | Yes, every note | No |
| Other doctors and staff | No | No |
| The patient | Never | No |

Private notes are never sent on WhatsApp, never appear on the [Records Portal](/patients/records-portal), and the AI receptionist doesn't read them.

<Info>
  If someone leaves your clinic, they lose access to their notes, but the notes stay on the patient with the author's name, so the owner can still read them.
</Info>

## Write a note from a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    In **Conversations**, open the patient's chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Private note">
    Above the message box, tap **Private note**. The box turns amber so you can't mistake it for a reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and save">
    Type the note and tap **Save** (or press Enter). It appears in the chat as a dashed amber bubble with a lock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can write a note even while the AI is on or the WhatsApp 24-hour window has closed. Tap **Reply** to go back to messaging the patient.

Use the **Private notes** filter at the top of the chat to see only notes.

## Notes on the patient profile

The **Private notes** card on the [patient profile](/patients/patient-profile) (and the quick look) lists every note you're allowed to see, newest first. You can add a note there too. The author can tap the pencil to edit a note or the bin to delete it.

## Private notes vs visit notes

| | Private notes | Visit notes |
|---|---|---|
| Where you write them | Chat or patient profile | The visit sheet in Appointments |
| Who sees them | Author, owner, admins | Your clinic team |
| Shown to the patient | Never | No |
