---
title: 'Widget Installation'
description: 'Copy your widget snippet and paste it into your website'
---

Installing the widget means pasting one `<script>` tag into your website. No other setup is needed.

## Step 1: Copy your snippet

Go to **Settings → Widget** and click **Copy** on the code card. The snippet already contains your clinic ID.

It looks like this:

```html
<script
  src="https://kashfy.site/widget/embed.js"
  data-clinic-id="YOUR_CLINIC_ID"
  data-lang="ar"
  async
></script>
```

<Tip>
  Always copy the snippet from your dashboard rather than typing it, so the clinic ID is exactly right.
</Tip>

| Attribute | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| `data-clinic-id` | Links the widget to your clinic. Required. |
| `data-lang` | `ar` for an Arabic widget (button on the left), `en` for English (button on the right). The snippet uses the language your dashboard was in when you copied it. |

Your clinic ID is safe to put in public website code. It only lets visitors chat with your AI, not access your dashboard.

## Step 2: Paste it before `</body>`

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="/images/icons/wordpress.svg" title="WordPress">
    Use a plugin like **WPCode** or **Insert Headers and Footers** and paste the snippet in the footer section. Or add it to your theme's `footer.php` just before `</body>`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="/images/icons/shopify.svg" title="Shopify">
    1. Go to **Online Store → Themes → Edit code**
    2. Open `layout/theme.liquid`
    3. Paste the snippet just before `</body>` and save
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="code" title="Custom HTML site">
    Paste the snippet just before the closing `</body>` tag on every page where you want the widget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Step 3: Check it works

Open your website. You should see the chat button in the bottom corner. Click it and ask a question, then check that the chat appears in **Conversations**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="eye-off" title="The button doesn't show">
    - Make sure the snippet is before `</body>` and was published
    - Make sure `data-clinic-id` wasn't changed
    - Try a private/incognito window to skip the browser cache
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="x-circle" title="The chat opens but the AI doesn't reply">
    - Check your plan: the widget doesn't answer on Solo plans
    - Check that your subscription is active
    - Complete your clinic profile (name, public URL, logo, description, phone and at least one address). The AI waits until these are filled in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="layers" title="It overlaps another chat button">
    Remove the other chat tool, or switch `data-lang` so the Kashfy button sits on the other side.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
