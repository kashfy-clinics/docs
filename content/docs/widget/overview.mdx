The widget is a floating chat button for your clinic's website. Visitors click it and chat with the same AI that answers your WhatsApp: they can book, ask about fees and services, and get your address without leaving your site.

<Info>
  The widget is available on the **Team plan and above**. On Solo plans the widget page shows an upgrade prompt, and the widget won't answer on your site.
</Info>

## What visitors see

- A round chat button in the bottom corner: **left** for the Arabic widget, **right** for the English one
- A chat panel (360 x 560 px on desktop). On phones it opens across the full width at the bottom of the screen.
- The header **Kashfy Assistant** and a greeting, in the language you chose when installing

## What visitors can do

- Book an appointment with any available doctor
- Check available times
- Ask about fees, services and anything in your [Knowledge Base](/ai-agent/knowledge-base)
- Get your clinic address

For privacy, the widget never sends medical records links or PINs, since it can't confirm who is typing. Those stay on WhatsApp.

## How visitors are identified

There is no login. Each visitor gets an anonymous session that lasts while their browser tab is open. Closing the tab starts a fresh conversation next time.

Widget chats appear in [Conversations](/patients/conversations) with a **Widget** label so your team can read them.

## Widget vs. WhatsApp

| | WhatsApp | Widget |
|---|----------|--------|
| Patient identified by | Phone number | Anonymous browser session |
| Conversation history | Kept across visits | Only while the tab is open |
| Staff can reply from Conversations | Yes (within 24 hours) | No, read only |
| Appointment reminders and review requests | Yes | No, there's no phone number |
| Records link and PIN | Yes | No |
| Setup | Share your [clinic code](/settings/codes-qr) | One script tag on your site |

<Tip>
  Use both. WhatsApp is where most patients are, and the widget catches people browsing your website.
</Tip>
