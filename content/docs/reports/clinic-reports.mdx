Clinic reports show how your clinic runs day to day: who came, how long patients waited, how full the schedule was and, for managers, where the money came from. Navigate to **Dashboard → Analytics** and open the **Clinic reports** tab.

## Period and filters

- **Period**: the same picker as the rest of Analytics (7, 30 or 90 days, or custom dates).
- **Branch**: follows the branch picked in the sidebar.
- **Provider**: **All providers** or one doctor.
- **Service**: **All services**, one procedure, or the visit fee or follow-up fee.

## Tiles

At the top: **Booked**, **Attendance**, **Median wait**, **Utilisation** and, for owners and admins, **Revenue**.

## Waiting time [#waiting-time]

**Waiting time** by branch and provider: the **Average wait** and the **Median wait**, and how many visits were measured.

The wait runs from the moment the patient arrives (checking in with the clinic QR, or reception marking them **Arrived**) to the doctor starting the visit. Visits without both times are left out.

<Info>
  Waiting time is counted from October 29, 2026, when this report shipped. Earlier visits have no start time, so they don't appear here.
</Info>

## Utilisation [#utilisation]

**Utilisation** by branch and provider: booked minutes against the minutes the schedule offers, with a chart per provider. The schedule's minutes follow the weekly hours, days off, custom hours and breaks.

For queue (walk-in) days, the report compares booked visits with the daily cap instead. Queue days without a cap are left out and labelled.

## Attendance [#attendance]

**Attendance by branch and provider**: booked, attended, cancelled, no-show, and **Attendance %**. Attendance % counts visits with a known outcome: came / (came + no-show).

## Revenue [#revenue]

Owners and admins also see:

- **Revenue by service**
- **Revenue by provider**
- **Revenue by referral source** (the patient's [How did you hear about us?](/settings/referral-source) answer)

Each shows visits, revenue and revenue per visit. A visit's payment goes to its procedures first (by price), and the rest to the visit fee.

<Note>
  Revenue here is what was collected for the visits in the period, after discounts. [Finance](/finance/revenue) counts payments by the day they were recorded, so the two can differ.
</Note>

## Branches side by side [#branches]

With two or more branches and **All branches** picked, **Branches side by side** puts each branch in its own column: booked, attended, no-show, cancelled, attendance %, average and median wait, utilisation % and, for managers, revenue and revenue per visit.

## Export [#export]

Use the buttons at the top to export the same tables:

- **CSV**
- **Excel**
- **PDF**: opens a print view; choose **Save as PDF**.
