The Reviews section is where patient ratings and comments land - one place to read what people are saying about your clinic and respond.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Reviews**.

## What a review looks like

Each review card shows:

- **Star rating** - 1 to 5 stars
- **Doctor** the review was left for
- **Patient name and phone** - so you can follow up if needed
- **Comment** - the patient's written feedback
- **Date** the review was submitted

Click the patient's name to open their record for a full history.

## Hiding a review

If a review is inappropriate, off-topic, or the patient asked you to remove it, toggle it hidden. Hidden reviews stay visible to you (with reduced opacity) but no longer count toward the doctor's public rating.

<Info>
  Kashfy doesn't let you edit or delete reviews - only hide them. This keeps the record honest: nothing is ever quietly rewritten.
</Info>

## Where reviews come from

Reviews come in through the same WhatsApp thread the AI agent uses. After an appointment, patients receive a follow-up asking for feedback. Their rating and comment flow straight into this page.

<Tip>
  Ratings drive doctor visibility in your website widget - highly-rated doctors are recommended first. Encourage happy patients to leave feedback.
</Tip>
