A campaign is one marketing message sent to all your opted-in patients. Each campaign becomes a WhatsApp template that Meta reviews before it can go out.

## Before you start

- You need credits that cover all your opted-in patients. See [Message Packs](/whatsapp-ads/packs).
- Only owners and admins can create campaigns.

## Create a campaign

Go to **WhatsApp Ads → New Campaign**. A live preview on the side shows how the message will look on a phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Campaign name">
    English lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only, for example `summer_checkup_offer`. Each name can be used once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message language">
    Pick Arabic or English. You'll get a warning if the text doesn't match the language you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Media (optional)">
    Add an image (JPG or PNG), a video (MP4) or a document (PDF). With media, you can't add a text header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Header (optional)">
    A short line above the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Body">
    The main message. Use the toolbar for emoji, *bold*, _italic_, ~strikethrough~ and monospace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Variables (optional)">
    Use **Add variable** (up to 9) for parts that change per message. Variables can't be the very first or last thing in the body. Fill in a sample for each: samples are only for Meta's review and are never sent to patients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Footer (optional)">
    A short line below the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buttons (optional)">
    Up to 3: **Call phone number**, **Visit website** (must start with `https://`), or **Quick Reply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recipients">
    Campaigns go to **All opted-in patients**. The current count is shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the policy and submit">
    The first time, tick that you agree to the WhatsApp Ads Policy. Then click **Submit for Review**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Kashfy checks your credits when you submit. If you don't have enough for all recipients, you'll see how many you have and how many the campaign needs.
</Warning>

## Campaign statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Awaiting Meta Approval** | Meta is reviewing the template |
| **Rejected by Meta** | Meta didn't approve it. Create a new campaign with adjusted content and a new name. |
| **Ready to Send** | Approved and ready to go out |
| **Sending** | Messages are going out now |
| **Sent** | Finished |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped and won't be sent |

The dashboard updates on its own, so you don't need to refresh.

<Tip>
  Meta approves faster when the message is clear, useful and not overly promotional. Avoid all-caps, misleading claims and vague text.
</Tip>

## Campaign details

Click a campaign to see:

- **Target**: how many patients it's addressed to
- **Sent**, **Failed** and **Skipped** counts
- **Credits** used
- A sending progress bar while it's going out
- The full message content (header, body, footer, buttons) and a preview
