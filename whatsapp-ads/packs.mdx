Campaign messages use credits. One credit = one message delivered to one patient. You buy credits in packs.

## Packs

| Pack | Messages | Price | Per message |
|------|----------|-------|-------------|
| Starter | 500 | 2,500 EGP | 5.00 EGP |
| Growth | 2,000 | 9,000 EGP | 4.50 EGP |
| Clinic (most popular) | 5,000 | 20,000 EGP | 4.00 EGP |

Every pack is valid for **12 months** from when it's activated. Unused credits expire with the pack. If you have more than one active pack, their credits add up.

## Buy a pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Buy Pack">
    On the **WhatsApp Ads** page (owners and admins only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a pack and submit">
    Check the order summary and click **Submit request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The confirmation shows the amount, a reference number and the payment details. Pay using one of the listed methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your payment proof">
    Use **Send payment proof on WhatsApp**. The message is pre-filled with your pack reference and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kashfy activates the pack">
    Once the payment is confirmed, the credits appear on your WhatsApp Ads page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can have one pack request waiting for payment at a time. Until it's paid, the WhatsApp Ads page shows a **Pack request awaiting payment** card with the payment details.
</Info>

## How credits are used

- One credit for each message successfully delivered
- Patients who haven't opted in are skipped and don't use credits
- A campaign can only be created if your remaining credits cover all its recipients
- If credits run out, campaigns stop until you buy a new pack
