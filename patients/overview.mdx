A patient record is created automatically the first time someone books, whether on WhatsApp, the widget, your public page, or a manual booking by your staff. Navigate to **Dashboard → Patients**.

## The patients list

Each row shows the patient's **name**, **phone**, **age** (when the date of birth is known), the **channel** they first came from (WhatsApp, Widget, or Manual), and the date they **joined**. The list is split into pages.

## Search and filters

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| **Search** | Name or phone |
| **Channel** | All channels, WhatsApp, Widget, Manual |
| **Gender** | All genders, Male, Female, Not specified |
| **Joined date** | Any date range |

## Quick look and full profile

Tap a patient to open a **quick look** panel with their details, alerts, and recent activity, without leaving the list. Tap **Profile** in the panel to open the full [Patient Profile](/patients/patient-profile).

<Info>
  There's no separate "add patient" form. To add someone new, book them an appointment. See [Booking manually](/appointments/overview#booking-an-appointment-manually).
</Info>

## Family members

Relatives who use the same phone number, such as children, can be added to one family from the patient's profile. Each family member has their own record and visits. See [Family](/patients/patient-profile#family).

## Who sees which patients

- Owners, admins, and employees see all patients.
- Doctors see only patients who have had a booking with them, under **My Patients** in the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal).

## How patients are identified

Patients are matched by phone number, so a returning WhatsApp patient lands on their existing record. Widget chats that never share a phone number may create a separate record.
