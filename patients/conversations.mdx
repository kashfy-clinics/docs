**Conversations** is your clinic's inbox. Every chat the AI has with patients on WhatsApp and the website widget lands here, live.

## The inbox

The list on the left shows one row per patient, newest activity first. Each row shows:

- Patient name (or **Unnamed**)
- A WhatsApp or widget icon
- The last message, with **AI:** or **You:** in front when it was a reply
- How long ago it was, and an unread count
- **AI paused** if the AI is off for this patient
- The doctor's name if the chat is assigned to one

Use the search box to find a patient by name or phone. Click a row to open the chat.

## Inside a chat

You see every message from the patient, the AI and your staff (with the staff member's name), plus timeline notes like "Sara disabled AI" or "Sara assigned Dr. Ahmed" so everyone knows who did what.

The header has the patient's name and number, a link to their details, and these controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **AI on/off switch** | Pause the AI to reply yourself, or turn it back on |
| **Assign** | Hand the chat to a doctor. The AI pauses and the doctor gets a notification. |
| **Unassign** | Remove the doctor. The AI starts replying again. |

## Replying to a patient

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause the AI">
    The reply box is locked while the AI is on, so you and the AI never answer at the same time. Flip the switch to pause it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your reply and press **Send** (or Enter). It goes to the patient on WhatsApp from the Kashfy number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the AI back on when done">
    Otherwise the AI stays paused for this patient, and their new messages wait for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can only reply to WhatsApp chats. Widget chats are read only from Conversations.
</Info>

If a message fails to send, it's removed from the chat and your text is put back in the box so you can try again.

## The 24-hour window

WhatsApp only allows normal messages within **24 hours of the patient's last message**. When that time has passed, the reply box is replaced with a notice:

> The patient's last message was more than 24 hours ago, and WhatsApp doesn't allow regular messages after that. You can reply here as soon as they message again.

There is no way to send a template from Conversations. This is on purpose: patients expect fast answers, so reply while the window is open. As soon as the patient writes again, the reply box comes back.

<Tip>
  Turn on [browser notifications](/settings/notifications) so you see new patient messages quickly, especially for chats where the AI is paused.
</Tip>

## When your team gets alerted

- A patient messages while the AI is paused for them
- A patient asks the AI to talk to a person (the AI pauses itself)
- A chat is assigned to a doctor (that doctor is notified)

Alerts for the same patient are grouped, so a burst of messages doesn't flood you.

## Doctors

Doctors see only the chats assigned to them, under **Conversations** in their own portal. They can reply and use **End conversation** to hand the chat back to the AI. Only owners, admins and staff can assign or unassign.

## What the AI remembers

The AI reads the recent messages in the chat before every reply, so patients don't have to repeat themselves. Very long chats are summarized automatically so the important context isn't lost.
