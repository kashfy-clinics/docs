Open a profile from **Dashboard → Patients** (tap a patient, then **Profile**), or tap **Patient record** on any visit sheet. Doctors open it from **My Patients** in the [Doctor Portal](/doctors/doctor-portal).

## Header

The patient's name, phone, and badges such as **Allergies** or **Family member**, with quick actions:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Call** | Dials the patient |
| **Message** | Opens the patient's chat |
| **Book** | Books a new appointment (not available to doctors) |
| **Edit** | Edits the patient's details |

Below it, three numbers at a glance: **Next visit**, **Last visit**, and **Attendance** (visits attended and no-shows). If the patient is on the waiting list, a notice says so.

## Medical alerts

**Allergies**, **chronic conditions**, and **current medications**. Doctors, owners, and admins tap **Edit** to update them (type and press Enter, or pick a suggestion like Penicillin or Diabetes). Anyone with allergies on file gets the **Allergies** badge in the header.

## Details

Date of birth and age, insurance company and ID, who referred them, the assigned doctor, whether they accept WhatsApp messages, and when they became a patient. Tap **Edit** to change the name, gender, date of birth, insurance, or referral.

## Family

Children or relatives seen on the same phone number. Tap **Add member** and enter a name, gender, and date of birth. Each member gets their own profile and visits; the account holder is marked **Account holder**.

## Tabs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="house" title="Overview">
    - **Quick briefing** - tap **Summarize** for a few AI-written lines covering allergies, conditions, recurring complaints, the latest prescription, pending tests, and attendance. Tap **Refresh** to update it. It's AI-generated, so double-check before relying on it.
    - **Visits** - every booking with date, doctor, and stage. Tap one to see the complaint, diagnosis, booking notes, chart notes, prescription, lab and scan orders, payment, and the patient's rating.
    - **Conversation** - the last message and whether the AI is replying or paused, with **Open chat**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="clock-rotate-left" title="Timeline">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="notes-medical" title="Medical">
    - **Prescriptions** - with **Open** and **Print** for each
    - **Lab & radiology** - tests and scans ordered during visits, and visits to your lab or radiology centre with a **Result** link once ready
    - **Files** - documents and images saved to the patient's file
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="receipt" title="Billing">
    **Total paid**, the number of completed visits with no payment, and every payment with discounts, insurance coverage, and voids. Not shown to doctors.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What doctors see

Doctors see the profile of any patient they've had a booking with, but only their own visits. They can update medical alerts and write clinical notes, and they don't see the Billing tab or the Book button.

## Patient's own access

Patients can view their own records, with a PIN, through the [Records Portal](/patients/records-portal).
