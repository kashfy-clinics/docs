The catalogue is your lab's price list. Staff pick tests from it on the intake board (prices fill in automatically), and the AI assistant uses it to answer patients on WhatsApp.

Go to **Tests catalogue** in the Lab sidebar.

<Info>
  Only owners and admins can add tests, change prices, or switch tests on and off. Other team members see the catalogue read-only.
</Info>

## Adding a test

Click **+ Add test** and fill in:

- **Arabic name** (required) - for example صورة دم كاملة
- **English name**
- **Code** - your internal code (optional)
- **Price (EGP)**
- **Turnaround (hrs)** - how long the result usually takes
- **Sample type** - for example venous blood, urine

Click **Save**.

## Switching a test off

Use the switch on a test to deactivate it when you stop offering it. Inactive tests no longer appear when staff search the catalogue on the intake board, and the AI stops quoting them. Old cards that used the test keep their details.

<Note>
  A saved test can't be edited from this screen yet. To change one test's name or price, switch it off and add it again with the right details. To change many prices at once, use **Adjust prices**.
</Note>

## Adjusting prices in bulk

Prices went up? **Adjust prices** changes every active test that has a price, by a percentage, in one step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Adjust prices">
    The **Bulk price adjustment** box opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a percentage">
    `10` raises prices by 10%. A negative number lowers them, for example `-5`. Allowed range: -90 to 500.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and confirm">
    Click **Apply**. Kashfy tells you how many tests will change and asks you to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Tests without a price and inactive tests are skipped. Prices change immediately.
</Warning>

## How the catalogue is used

- **At intake** - in **New walk-in** or a card's **Edit**, type part of a test name to search the catalogue. Picking a test adds it with its price, and the total updates. Anything not in the catalogue can still be added as free text.
- **On WhatsApp** - when a patient asks about a test or its price, the AI assistant answers only from your active tests and prices, and can register the patient. Their card appears on the board with a **Booked** badge. The AI doesn't give preparation instructions (like fasting); it tells the patient the lab will confirm.

Keep prices current: a stale price here is a wrong quote to a patient.
