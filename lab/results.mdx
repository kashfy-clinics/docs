---
title: 'Results'
description: 'Upload lab result files and send them to patients on WhatsApp automatically'
---

The Results page lists every intake whose status is **Ready** - meaning the test has run and the report needs to go out to the patient.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Lab → Results**.

## Sending a result

Each ready intake shows the patient's name, phone, tests, and the time they were registered.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach the result file">
    Upload the result PDF (or image) to the intake card. Kashfy stores it against the patient's record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kashfy sends it on WhatsApp">
    As soon as the file is uploaded, it goes out on WhatsApp with a short cover message. No manual send step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status moves to Delivered">
    The intake disappears from the Results queue and is marked delivered in the patient's history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If the patient didn't provide a phone number at intake, the result file is stored but not sent. You'll need to hand it over in person or add a phone later.
</Info>

## Viewing past results

Once an intake is marked delivered, its result file stays attached to the patient's record. Open the patient from **Patients** to see every historical result.
