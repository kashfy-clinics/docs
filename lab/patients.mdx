The **Patients** page in the Lab or Radiology sidebar lists everyone who has had a card on your intake board. It works the same in both workspaces and includes both lab and radiology visits.

## Summary

Three cards at the top: **Patients**, **Visits**, and **Revenue**.

## The list

Each patient appears once (matched by mobile number, or by name when there's no number) with:

- Name and mobile (tap the number to call)
- Total spent in EGP - counts **completed** cases only
- Number of **lab** and **rad** visits - every card counts as a visit, finished or not
- **Last visit** date
- **Open chat** - jumps to the patient's WhatsApp conversation in **Conversations**

Use **Search by name or phone** to find someone.

<Tip>
  To see or resend a specific result, use the [Results](/lab/results) page.
</Tip>
