title: 'QR check-in'
description: 'Patients scan a QR at your counter, send one WhatsApp message, and appear on the intake board'

QR check-in turns the patient's phone into your reception terminal. They scan the QR at your counter, WhatsApp opens with a ready message, they tap **Send**, and their card appears on your [intake queue](/lab/intake) in **Arrived**. No typing for your staff.

It works for both **Lab** and **Radiology**. Each branch has its own QR per service, so every scan lands on the right branch's board.

## Getting your QR

Every lab or radiology branch gets its check-in QR codes automatically. You don't need to contact support.

Print them from **Settings → Organization → Codes & QR → Branch check-in QR**. See [Codes & QR](/settings/codes-qr) for details.

<Info>
  All Kashfy centers use one shared Kashfy WhatsApp number, so there is nothing to connect before QR check-in works.
</Info>

## What the patient does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scans the QR">
    Any phone camera opens it. WhatsApp opens with a message that contains a one-time 6-character code (for example `الكود التعريفي بتاعي هو K7Q2MX`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Taps Send">
    Kashfy reads the code and knows exactly which branch and service (lab or radiology) the patient scanned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gets a welcome message">
    The patient gets a welcome on WhatsApp confirming their arrival and asking them to send a photo of their prescription. If you already have their tests (for example from an AI booking) or their prescription, the welcome just confirms check-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sends the prescription photo">
    The photo attaches to their card. Staff see it in the card's **Prescription** section, and the card shows a paperclip with the number of files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What staff see

The card appears on the board right away, without refreshing, with:

- **Patient name** - from their existing patient record, or their WhatsApp profile name
- **Mobile number** - captured from WhatsApp, so the result can be sent there later
- **Arrival time** and **branch**

You then pick the tests or scans, confirm the total, and move the card along as usual.

## Rescans and AI bookings

- **The code is valid for 24 hours** after scanning, so a patient who scans and sends later still checks in.
- **Scanning again doesn't create a duplicate.** If the patient already has an open card at your center, Kashfy reuses it and moves it to the branch they scanned at. A card already **In progress** keeps its stage.
- **AI bookings are matched on scan.** A patient who booked with the AI assistant on WhatsApp has a **Booked** card. When they scan at the counter, that card is marked arrived and moved to the scanned branch.

## When check-in doesn't happen

- If the branch is deactivated or the center's account is suspended, the scan does not create a card. The patient's message is answered by the normal WhatsApp assistant instead.
- If the QR link is broken or unknown, the patient lands on the Kashfy website rather than an error page.

## Patients who can't scan

Add them with **New walk-in** on the [intake queue](/lab/intake). Scanned and manual cards live on the same board.
