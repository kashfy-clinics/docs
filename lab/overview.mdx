The Lab workspace is built for labs and clinics that run their own lab. Patients check in at the counter (by scanning a QR or being added by staff), you move them through a simple three-column board, and when you upload the result Kashfy sends it to the patient on WhatsApp.

<Info>
  The Lab workspace only appears if your organization has the **Lab** type. The owner can add it from **Settings → Organization → Organisation Types → Enable**. Adding a type does not change your subscription or billing. Removing a type needs Kashfy support.
</Info>

## The lab dashboard

Go to **Lab dashboard** in the sidebar to see the day at a glance:

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Today's total** | Lab cards created today |
| **Waiting** | Cards still on the board (Arrived or In progress) |
| **Completed today** | Cards whose result was uploaded today |

## A typical lab day

<Steps>
  <Step title="The patient checks in">
    They scan your branch's [check-in QR](/lab/qr-intake) and send the WhatsApp message, or reception adds them with **New walk-in**. Patients who booked with the AI assistant on WhatsApp are already on the board with a **Booked** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the prescription and confirm the tests">
    Prescription photos the patient sends on WhatsApp attach to their card. Open the card to pick the tests from your catalogue and confirm the total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start processing">
    Move the card to **In progress** when you start on the sample.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the result">
    Choose the result file and confirm. The card moves to **Complete** and the result goes to the patient on WhatsApp. If a [referring doctor](/lab/referrals) was added with the patient's consent, they get it too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Patients can also ask the AI assistant on WhatsApp "is my result ready?" (نتيجتي جاهزة؟). If the result is uploaded, the AI sends them their secure result link.
</Tip>
