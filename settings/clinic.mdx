Clinic settings control how your clinic is presented to patients and how the AI represents you in conversations. Keep this information accurate — the AI pulls from it constantly.

## Accessing clinic settings

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Clinic**.

## General information

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="building" title="Clinic name">
    Your clinic's name in Arabic and English. The AI uses the Arabic name in patient conversations. Both are required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="stethoscope" title="Specialty">
    The primary medical specialty of your clinic. Helps the AI contextualize conversations ("We are a dermatology clinic...").
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="phone" title="Phone number">
    The phone number the AI shares when patients ask to speak to someone directly. This should be your clinic's public-facing phone line.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="align-left" title="Description">
    A short description of your clinic in Arabic and English. The AI may use this when patients ask general questions about your services.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="image" title="Logo">
    Upload your clinic's logo. It appears on the public-facing clinic page and (optionally) in the website widget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Address & locations

Your clinic's physical address is set here. For multi-branch clinics, each branch has its own address.

- **Street address** — Full address including building number and street name
- **Area** — Select from the list of areas (used for patient-facing directions and search)
- **Google Maps link** — Optionally add a Google Maps URL so the AI can share a direct link with patients asking for directions

## Working hours

Set your clinic's overall operating hours. Individual doctor schedules are set separately (in **Dashboard → Doctors**) but cannot extend beyond the clinic's working hours.

## Clinic readiness

The **Readiness** section shows you a checklist of what's been configured and what's still missing before you can go live. Items include:

- Clinic profile complete
- At least one active doctor with working hours
- WhatsApp connected (or widget installed)
- Active subscription

<Info>
  The AI will not handle patient bookings until your clinic is considered "ready." You can still test it in the AI test chat regardless.
</Info>
