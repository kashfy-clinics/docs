Kashfy is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your device so it opens in its own window — no browser tab, no address bar, just the dashboard.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → App** to find device-specific installation instructions.

## Desktop installation (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

1. Look for the **install icon** in your browser's address bar (a small download/computer icon on the right side).
2. Click it and choose **Install**.
3. Kashfy opens in its own window from that point on.

<Note>
  Firefox and Safari do not support PWA desktop installation. Use Chrome, Edge, or Brave for the best experience.
</Note>

If you don't see the install icon, the browser may have already prompted you previously. Check **Settings → Apps** in Chrome or Edge to see if Kashfy is already installed.

## Mobile installation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="apple" title="iOS (Safari)">
    1. Open Kashfy in **Safari** (must be Safari — other iOS browsers don't support Add to Home Screen).
    2. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
    4. Confirm the name and tap **Add**.

    Kashfy now appears on your home screen and opens in a full-screen view.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="android" title="Android (Chrome)">
    1. Open the browser **menu** (three dots in the top right).
    2. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.
    3. Confirm.

    Some Android browsers show a banner at the bottom of the screen — you can tap that too.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
