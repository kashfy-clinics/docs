Kashfy has three organization types: **Clinic**, **Lab**, and **Radiology Centre**. You pick them during onboarding, and you can add more later. Each type adds its own section to the dashboard.

## Enabling a module

<Info>
  Only the **owner** can enable a module. Other roles see a **Contact us** link instead.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organisation Types">
    Go to **Dashboard → Settings → Clinic** and open **Organisation Types** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enable">
    Active types show an **Active** badge. Click **Enable** on the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The module is added right away and its section appears in the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Adding a module has no effect on your subscription or billing.

## Removing a module

Removal isn't self-serve. Contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site).

## What each module adds

| Module | Sidebar sections |
|--------|------------------|
| **Clinic** | Dashboard, AI Agent, Appointments, Patients, Conversations, Doctors, Services, Knowledge, Finance, Reviews |
| **Lab** | Lab dashboard, Intake queue, Results, Tests catalogue, Patients, Finance, Conversations |
| **Radiology Centre** | Radiology dashboard, Intake queue, Reports, Scans catalogue, Patients, Finance, Conversations |

When you run more than one type, a switcher in the sidebar moves between them.
