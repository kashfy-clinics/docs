Kashfy supports three organisation types: **Clinic**, **Lab**, and **Radiology Centre**. Each type unlocks a dedicated module in the dashboard.

By default, your organisation is set up as a Clinic. If your facility also runs a lab, a radiology unit, or operates as a standalone lab or radiology centre, you can enable the relevant modules from settings.

## Enabling a module

<Info>
  Only the **organisation owner** can enable new modules. Managers and staff will see a "Contact us" link instead.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Organisation Settings">
    Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Clinic** and scroll to the **Organisation Types** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the module you want to enable">
    You'll see cards for **Clinic**, **Lab**, and **Radiology Centre**. Active modules are marked with an "Active" badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enable">
    Click **Enable** on the module you want to add. A confirmation prompt will appear: "This will add the module to your organisation. Removal requires contacting support."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click to confirm. The module is activated immediately and its section appears in the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disabling a module

Module removal is not self-serve. To disable a module, contact support at [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site).

## What each module adds

| Module | Dashboard sections added |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Clinic** | Appointments, Doctors, Patients, AI Agent, Services |
| **Lab** | Lab Intake, Lab Catalogue, Lab Results |
| **Radiology Centre** | Radiology Intake, Radiology Catalogue, Radiology Results |
