title: 'Insurance & Partners'
description: 'Choose which insurance companies each lab or radiology branch accepts'
---

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Insurance & Partners**.

<Info>
  This page only appears for organizations with a **lab** or **radiology centre**, and only lists branches that offer lab or radiology. For clinic visits, choose your insurers in the [Insurance section of Organization Settings](/settings/organization#insurance).
</Info>

## Choosing insurers per branch

1. Find the branch in the list. Each one shows how many insurers it accepts.
2. Toggle the insurance companies that branch works with.
3. Tap **Save** for that branch.

Accepted insurers are then offered at intake, so reception can record the patient's insurance.

## Who can edit

Owners and admins can change these settings. Other roles can view them.

## Missing a company?

The provider list is managed by Kashfy. If a company you work with is missing, contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site).
