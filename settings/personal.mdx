title: 'Personal Settings'
description: 'Update your personal account details — name, profile photo, and phone number'
---

Personal settings control your own account profile within Kashfy. These settings are per-user and do not affect your clinic or your team members.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Personal**.

## What you can update

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="user" title="Full name">
    Your display name shown across the dashboard and in team member lists. This is separate from your clinic's name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="image" title="Profile photo">
    Upload a profile picture. Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP. Recommended size: at least 256 × 256 px.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="phone" title="Phone number">
    Your personal phone number. This is used for your account only — it is not your clinic's public phone number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Email address

Your email address is shown but **cannot be changed** from this page. It is set when you create your account. Contact support if you need to update it.

## Demo accounts

Personal settings editing is disabled on the demo account (`demo@kashfy.test`).
