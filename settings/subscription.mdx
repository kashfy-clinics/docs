The Subscription page is your real-time view of what you're on and how much of it you've used this month. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Subscription**.

## Current plan

Your active plan is shown at the top, including:

- Plan name and tagline
- Monthly price in EGP
- Included bookings per month
- Maximum number of doctors
- Overage rate per extra booking (EGP per booking above the quota)

Use the **View plans** button to compare plans or upgrade.

## This month's usage

### Bookings

A progress bar shows how many confirmed bookings you've made this month against your plan's included quota.

| Usage level | Bar color |
|-------------|-----------|
| Under 80% | Green (primary) |
| 80 - 99% | Amber |
| 100%+ | Red |

The cost breakdown below the bar shows:
- **Plan fee** — your fixed monthly subscription
- **Extra bookings** (if any) — number of bookings over quota × overage rate
- **Projected this month** — total estimated charge at current pace

### AI messages

A second progress bar tracks the number of messages your AI agent has processed this month. The monthly cap is calculated as **7 × included bookings** — so a plan with 200 included bookings has a 1,400 message cap.

<Warning>
  If the AI message cap is reached, the AI assistant is paused for the remainder of the month. The bar turns red and a warning is displayed. Upgrade your plan or wait for the next billing period to resume.
</Warning>

## Latest invoice

A summary of your most recent invoice is shown at the bottom — period, total bookings, amount, and payment status.

For your full invoice history, go to [Billing settings](/settings/billing).
