Settings in Kashfy are organized into sections, accessible from the left sidebar on desktop or a horizontal tab strip on mobile. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings** to get started.

## Settings sections

| Section | Who sees it | What it controls |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Personal** | Everyone | Your name, avatar, and phone number |
| **Organization** | Everyone | Clinic/lab profile, addresses, working hours, branding |
| **Modules** | Owner only | Enable Lab or Radiology Centre modules |
| **Insurance & Partners** | Labs and radiology centres | Which insurance companies each branch accepts |
| **Widget** | Clinic plan (Team+) | Website chat widget installation and customization |
| **Notifications** | Everyone | Browser push and WhatsApp notification preferences |
| **App** | Everyone | Install Kashfy as a PWA on your device |
| **Subscription** | Everyone | Current plan, monthly usage, and projected cost |
| **Billing** | Everyone | Invoice history |
| **Team** | Everyone | Invite and manage team members |

<Info>
  The **Organization** section label changes based on your account type — it shows "Clinic" for clinic accounts, "Lab" for labs, and "Radiology Centre" for radiology accounts.
</Info>

<Info>
  **Insurance & Partners** only appears for accounts that include a lab or radiology centre. It is not shown for clinic-only accounts.
</Info>

## Navigating settings

On desktop, the settings sidebar stays fixed on the left while the content scrolls independently. On mobile, settings sections appear as a horizontally scrollable tab strip at the top.

Settings always opens on **Personal** by default.
