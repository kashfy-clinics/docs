Kashfy alerts your team when something needs attention. Alerts show in the dashboard bell, and can also come as browser notifications and a sound.

Go to **Settings → Notifications**.

<Info>
  These settings are **per device**. Turning on notifications on your laptop doesn't turn them on for your phone or for a colleague. Each person sets them up on each device they use.
</Info>

## The two options

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Browser notifications** | Pop-up notifications even when the Kashfy tab is in the background |
| **Sound alerts** | A beep on new bookings |

Turn on what you want and click **Save**. When you turn on browser notifications for the first time, your browser asks for permission: click **Allow**.

<Warning>
  If you see "Notifications are blocked", your browser or phone refused permission earlier. Allow notifications for the Kashfy site in your browser or system settings, then save again.
</Warning>

The first time you open the dashboard on a new device, Kashfy shows an **Enable notifications** prompt with the same two options. Pick **Enable** or **Not now**, and you can change it here any time.

<Tip>
  Installing Kashfy as an app on your phone makes notifications more reliable. See [App](/settings/app).
</Tip>

## What you get notified about

| Event | Who gets it |
|-------|-------------|
| New booking (by the AI, your team, or your public page) | Owners, admins, staff, and the doctor of the appointment |
| A patient cancels through the AI | Owners, admins, staff, and the doctor |
| A patient messages while the AI is paused for them | Owners, admins and staff, plus the assigned doctor |
| A patient asks to talk to a person | Owners, admins and staff, plus the assigned doctor |
| A chat is assigned to a doctor | That doctor |
| New lab or radiology registration by the AI | Owners, admins and staff |

Message alerts for the same patient are grouped, so you won't get one for every message in a burst.

## Patient reminders are separate

Reminders that patients receive before their appointment are not team notifications. Set them up in [Appointment Reminders](/appointments/reminders).
