Go to **Settings → Widget**. This page appears for clinics and has two parts.

## Instagram messages

Connect an Instagram business account so the AI also replies to your Instagram direct messages.

- Click **Connect Instagram** and approve access on Instagram's screen
- Once connected, the card shows a check mark and when the connection renews (it renews automatically)
- Use **Reconnect** if the connection ever needs refreshing
- Only the owner or an admin can connect
- An Instagram account can only be connected to one clinic on Kashfy

## Website widget snippet

The code card shows your ready-to-paste snippet with a **Copy** button. Paste it just before the closing `</body>` tag on your website. Full steps for WordPress, Wix and Shopify are in [Widget Installation](/widget/installation).

<Info>
  The website widget is available on the **Team plan and above**. On Solo plans this part of the page shows an upgrade prompt with a **View plans** button instead of the snippet. Instagram is shown on every plan.
</Info>

The widget's look (button, colors, greeting) is fixed. The only option is the language, set by `data-lang` in the snippet.
