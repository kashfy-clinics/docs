The Radiology workspace works like the [Lab workspace](/lab/overview), for X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI, and other scans. Patients check in at the counter, you move them through a three-column board, and when you upload the report Kashfy sends it to the patient on WhatsApp.

<Info>
  The Radiology workspace only appears if your organization has the **Radiology** type. The owner can add it from **Settings → Organization → Organisation Types → Enable**. Adding a type does not change your subscription or billing. Removing a type needs Kashfy support.
</Info>

## The radiology dashboard

Go to **Radiology dashboard** in the sidebar:

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Today's total** | Radiology cards created today |
| **Waiting** | Cards still on the board (Arrived or In progress) |
| **Completed today** | Cards whose report was uploaded today |

## A typical day

<Steps>
  <Step title="The patient checks in">
    They scan the branch's radiology [check-in QR](/lab/qr-intake), or reception adds them with **New walk-in**. AI bookings from WhatsApp are already on the board with a **Booked** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the scans">
    Open the card, check the prescription photo the patient sent, pick the scans from your catalogue, and confirm the total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start processing">
    Move the card to **In progress** when the scan starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the report">
    Choose the report file and confirm. The card moves to **Complete** and the report goes to the patient on WhatsApp, and to the [referring doctor](/lab/referrals) if the patient agreed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your organization runs both a lab and radiology, the intake queue shows both kinds of cards on one board, each with its own badge.
</Tip>
