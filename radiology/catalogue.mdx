The catalogue is your radiology center's price list. Staff pick scans from it on the intake board (prices fill in automatically), and the AI assistant uses it to answer patients on WhatsApp.

Go to **Scans catalogue** in the Radiology sidebar.

<Info>
  Only owners and admins can add scans, change prices, or switch scans on and off. Other team members see the catalogue read-only.
</Info>

## Adding a scan

Click **+ Add scan** and fill in:

- **Arabic name** (required) - for example أشعة صدر
- **English name**
- **Code** - your internal code (optional)
- **Price (EGP)**
- **Modality** - for example X-ray, MRI, CT
- **Body part** - for example chest, lumbar spine

Click **Save**.

## Switching a scan off

Use the switch on a scan to deactivate it when you stop offering it. Inactive scans no longer appear when staff search the catalogue on the intake board, and the AI stops quoting them. Old cards keep their details.

<Note>
  A saved scan can't be edited from this screen yet. To change one scan's name or price, switch it off and add it again. To change many prices at once, use **Adjust prices**.
</Note>

## Adjusting prices in bulk

Click **Adjust prices**, enter a percentage (`10` for +10%, `-5` for -5%, allowed range -90 to 500), click **Apply**, and confirm. Only active scans that have a price are changed.

<Warning>
  Prices change immediately. Kashfy shows how many scans will change before you confirm.
</Warning>

## How the catalogue is used

- **At intake** - type part of a scan name in **New walk-in** or a card's **Edit** to pick it with its price. Scans not in the catalogue can be added as free text.
- **On WhatsApp** - the AI assistant quotes only your active scans and prices, and can register the patient (their card shows **Booked**). It never gives preparation instructions (like fasting or contrast); it tells the patient the center will confirm.
