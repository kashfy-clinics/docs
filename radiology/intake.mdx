The intake queue lists every walk-in for imaging. Each row is a patient with their scans, total, and current stage.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Radiology → Intake queue**.

## Adding an intake manually

Click **New intake** to open the form and fill in:

- **Patient name** (required)
- **Patient phone** - used to send the report on WhatsApp
- **Scans** - the scans being performed (chest X-ray, abdomen ultrasound, etc.)
- **Total (EGP)** - price the patient is paying; auto-filled from catalogue when picking from the menu
- **Notes** - technician notes: contrast used, prior scans, referring doctor

## The status cycle

Tap the status pill to advance the intake:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Registered, scan not yet performed |
| **Collected** | Patient prepped, scan starting |
| **Processing** | Scan complete, radiologist reviewing |
| **Ready** | Report is written - upload it to send to the patient |
| **Delivered** | Report has been sent/handed over |

## QR arrivals

If your branch has a printed QR sticker, patients scanning it are added here automatically with all their details captured. Cards from QR scans show a QR badge to distinguish them from manual entries. See [QR intake](/lab/qr-intake) for the full flow.
