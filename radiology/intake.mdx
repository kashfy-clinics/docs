Radiology uses the same intake board as the lab. Go to **Intake queue** in the Radiology sidebar. For the full guide, see [Intake queue](/lab/intake).

## The columns

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Arrived** | The patient is at the center (or booked with the AI). The scan hasn't started. |
| **In progress** | The scan is being done or the report is being written. |
| **Complete** | The report was uploaded and sent. |

Cards are oldest arrival first and update live. The board shows every open card plus those completed today. Cards with a **Booked** badge came from the AI assistant on WhatsApp.

## Adding a walk-in

Click **New walk-in** (choose **Radiology** if your organization also runs a lab) and fill in the patient name, mobile number, the **Scans** from your [catalogue](/radiology/catalogue), the total, and any notes such as contrast or prior scans. Add the [referring doctor](/lab/referrals) here too.

## Completing a scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start processing">
    Open the card and click **Start processing**, or use the arrow on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the report">
    Click **Choose result file** (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC, up to 20 MB), check the details, and click **Upload & send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The report goes to the patient on WhatsApp. See [Reports](/radiology/results) for how delivery works.

## Other things you can do on a card

- **Edit** the patient's name, mobile, or referring doctor at any stage
- **Edit** the requested scans and total
- See the **prescription** photos the patient sent
- **Remove** a card that's still in Arrived (a mistaken scan or no-show)
- Move a completed card **Back** to upload a corrected report
