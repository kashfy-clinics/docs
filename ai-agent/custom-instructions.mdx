Custom instructions are your clinic's own rules for the AI: what to always ask, what never to say, how to handle special cases. You set them by chatting, not by filling in a form.

## Where to set them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Agent">
    Click **AI Agent** in the sidebar. Only owners and admins see this page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the Training tab">
    The **Patient** tab is for testing as a patient. **Training** is for teaching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it the rule in plain words">
    Write in Arabic or English, for example "خلي الـ Agent يسأل عن الحساسية دايماً" or "Always ask about allergies". The trainer saves the rule and confirms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Go back to the **Patient** tab and try a conversation where the rule should apply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Useful things to say in Training:

| You say | What happens |
|---------|--------------|
| "Show me the current instructions" | It shows everything saved so far |
| "Always ask about allergies" | Adds a rule |
| "Remove the rule about parking" | Edits the saved rules |
| "Clear all custom instructions" | Deletes all rules |

<Info>
  Rules are stored in English, whatever language you used, and the AI still replies to patients in their own language. All rules together can be up to 4,000 characters.
</Info>

## Good rules to add

```
- If a doctor the patient asks for isn't available, suggest another doctor in the same specialty
- Mention that we accept installments when patients ask about prices
- Don't book anything less than one hour from now
- If a patient describes an emergency, tell them to call 123 right away
```

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="list" title="Be specific">
    "Be polite" adds nothing, the AI already is. "Mention our free parking when a patient is choosing between branches" is useful.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-slash" title="Say what not to do">
    Restrictions like "don't mention other clinics" work well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="book" title="Facts go in the Knowledge Base">
    Prices, insurance lists and FAQs belong in the [Knowledge Base](/ai-agent/knowledge-base). Instructions are for behavior.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What instructions can't change

- The AI will not give medical advice or diagnoses
- It always uses real availability, so it can't offer times your doctors don't have
- It won't send records links or PINs on the website widget
