title: 'Services'

---

Services are the procedures you offer besides the consultation, such as ECG, dressings, or injections. The AI uses this list to tell patients whether you do something and what it costs.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Services**. Owners and admins can add and edit services; other roles can view them.

## Adding a service

Tap **Add service**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Open **Select a service** and search the shared catalogue in English or Arabic. Services you already added aren't shown.

    Not in the list? Type the name and tap **Add "[name]" as new service**, then enter the **English** and **Arabic** names. New services are only visible to your clinic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price">
    Enter the price in EGP, or tick **Included in visit** if the consultation fee already covers it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add">
    The service appears in your list straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a price

Tap the price on any service. Change the amount, or tick or untick **Included in visit**, then save.

## Removing a service

Tap the remove icon on the service and confirm.

## What the AI does with services

When a patient asks "بتعملوا ايكو؟", the AI checks this list to answer and quote the price. If a service isn't listed, the AI says you don't offer it, so add everything you actually do, even rare procedures.

<Tip>
  Mark add-ons your visit price already covers as **Included in visit** so the AI doesn't quote an extra charge.
</Tip>

## Services vs. lab and radiology

Services are procedures done during a doctor's visit. Lab tests and scans have their own catalogues with extra details. See the [Lab catalogue](/lab/catalogue) and [Radiology catalogue](/radiology/catalogue).
