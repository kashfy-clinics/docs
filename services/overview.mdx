The Services module is your clinic's menu of add-on services — things you charge for on top of (or instead of) a plain consultation. Setting these up lets the AI agent quote accurate prices and lets staff bill quickly.

Navigate to **Dashboard → Services**.

## Adding a service

Click **Add service** (the **+** button) to open the add form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick from the catalogue or create new">
    Click the **Select a service** dropdown. Type to search the shared catalogue by English or Arabic name. If your service is in the list, select it — the bilingual name is already filled in.

    If your service isn't in the catalogue, type its name and click **Add "[name]" as new service** at the bottom of the dropdown. You'll then enter both an **Arabic name** and an **English name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price">
    Choose one of two pricing options:

    - **Set a price** — enter the amount in EGP. This is what the AI quotes patients when they ask.
    - **Included in visit** — tick the checkbox. No extra charge; the service is covered by the consultation fee. The price field disables automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**. The service appears in your list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing prices

Prices can be updated at any time directly from the services list:

1. Click the price badge on any service.
2. Type the new amount.
3. Press **✓** to save or **✗** to cancel.

To change a service from priced to "included in visit" (or vice versa), remove it and re-add it.

## Removing a service

Click the **trash icon** on any service card. Confirm the prompt to remove it from your clinic's menu.

## What the AI does with services

When a patient asks "بعمل ايكو؟" (do you do echocardiograms?) the AI checks this list to answer yes/no and quote the price. If a service isn't in the list, the AI says it doesn't offer that service — so add anything you actually do here, even if it's rare.

<Tip>
  Mark diagnostic add-ons like ECG or blood pressure as "included in visit" if your consultation fee covers them — it keeps the AI from double-charging patients when they ask about costs.
</Tip>

## Services vs. Lab / Radiology

Services are for clinical procedures done during a doctor visit — dressings, ECG, injections, minor procedures. For lab tests and radiology scans, use the dedicated [Lab catalogue](/lab/catalogue) and [Radiology catalogue](/radiology/catalogue) instead — they carry extra fields like turnaround time and modality.
