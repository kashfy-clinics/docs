Kashfy offers monthly and yearly subscription plans based on the number of bookings your clinic needs. All plans include the full feature set — the difference is in booking volume and the number of doctors you can add.

## Free trial

Every new clinic starts on a **7-day free trial** with no credit card required. During the trial, you have access to all features with a limited booking quota so you can test the AI with real patients before committing.

<Tip>
  Use the trial period to connect WhatsApp, add your doctors, and test the AI with real patient conversations.
</Tip>

## Plans overview

Plans are priced in **Egyptian Pounds (EGP)** and billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing includes a discount compared to paying month by month.

Each plan includes:

- **Included bookings per month** — the number of AI-confirmed appointments per billing cycle
- **Maximum doctors** — how many doctors can be active on your account
- **All core features** — AI agent, WhatsApp, widget, dashboard, team management, knowledge base

## What counts as a booking

A booking is counted when the AI successfully creates an appointment. Bookings that are later **cancelled** are **not counted** — your quota is only consumed by active, non-cancelled appointments.

| Event | Counts toward quota? |
|-------|---------------------|
| AI creates an appointment | Yes |
| Appointment is later cancelled | Deducted (refunded) |
| Appointment is no-show | Yes |
| Appointment is completed | Yes |
| Manually created appointment | Yes |

## Overage fees

If your clinic exceeds the included bookings in your plan, you are charged an **overage fee per booking** above the limit. Overage fees are billed at the end of the monthly cycle as part of your invoice.

To avoid overages, upgrade to a higher plan if your booking volume consistently exceeds your current plan.

## Upgrading or downgrading

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Subscription** to change your plan:

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately — your new quota applies right away
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle

## Yearly vs. monthly billing

| Billing | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly | Flexible, cancel anytime |
| Yearly | Lower effective monthly cost |

Switching from monthly to yearly can be done at any time from the subscription settings. The transition is prorated.
