Every plan includes the AI agent, WhatsApp booking, the knowledge base, and the full dashboard. Plans differ in included bookings, number of doctors, and the website widget.

## Free trial

Your **first 30 bookings are free**. No card and no time limit.

- From 20 bookings, a banner shows how many free bookings are left.
- At 30, the AI assistant pauses and the dashboard asks you to choose a plan.
- The first time a patient messages you after that, Kashfy opens a **one-time grace period**: the AI keeps working for **7 days or 20 more bookings**, whichever comes first.
- After the grace period the AI stays paused until a plan is activated.

<Info>
  The free trial is once per owner. If your verified mobile number already started a trial for another organization, a new organization starts with the trial and grace period already used. During the trial the AI also stops after 300 WhatsApp conversations.
</Info>

## What counts as a booking

A booking is counted once, the first time the visit actually happens. It doesn't matter whether the AI, the widget, or your staff created it.

| Event | Counted? |
|-------|----------|
| Appointment reserved | Not yet |
| Patient marked **Arrived**, **In progress**, or **Completed** | Yes |
| Appointment cancelled or no-show (never arrived) | No |
| Lab or radiology result delivered | Yes |

Once a booking is counted it stays counted, even if its status changes later.

## Extra bookings

Bookings above your plan's included amount are charged at your plan's extra-booking price on the next invoice. The [Subscription](/settings/subscription) page shows your projected total as you go.

## AI fair-use cap

The AI handles up to **7 times your monthly included bookings** in patient messages each month (for example, 2,100 messages on monthly Solo). When it's reached, the AI pauses until the month ends.

## Activating or changing a plan

There is no online payment. To activate a plan:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Open **Settings → Subscription → View plans** (or follow the **Choose a plan** banner) and tap **Contact us to activate**. This opens WhatsApp with a message to the Kashfy team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the plan fee">
    By InstaPay, mobile wallet, or bank transfer. See [Payments](/billing/payments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the receipt">
    Send the transfer receipt on WhatsApp. The team activates your plan, usually the same day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To switch plans later, contact the Kashfy team the same way.
