Kashfy has **no online card payment**. You pay by transfer, send the receipt on WhatsApp, and the Kashfy team confirms it. All amounts are in **Egyptian Pounds (EGP)**.

## Paying an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Go to **Dashboard → Settings → Billing**. Every unpaid invoice has a **How to pay** box under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer the amount">
    Use any method listed in the box: **InstaPay**, **mobile wallet**, or **bank transfer**. Each detail has a copy button. Write the invoice **reference** (for example `KSH-1A2B3C4D`) in the transfer notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the receipt">
    Tap **Send payment proof on WhatsApp**. It opens a chat with the Kashfy team with the reference and amount already filled in. Attach the transfer screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    The team checks the transfer and marks the invoice **Paid**. If your account was suspended, service resumes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If no payment details are listed in the box, message the team on WhatsApp and they'll send them.
</Info>

## Paying to activate a plan

Activation works the same way: pick a plan in **View plans**, pay the plan fee by transfer, and send the receipt on WhatsApp. See [Activating a plan](/billing/plans#activating-or-changing-a-plan).

## Cancelling your subscription

Owners can tap **Request cancellation** on the [Subscription](/settings/subscription#cancel-subscription) page. The team contacts you to settle the last invoice and stop the subscription.
