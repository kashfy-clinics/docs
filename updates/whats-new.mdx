Stay up to date with the latest improvements to Kashfy. Major updates are listed here with a summary of what changed and why.

---

## August 2025

### AI agent improvements
- Improved understanding of Egyptian dialect edge cases in appointment booking conversations
- Better handling of multi-doctor requests ("بكرة في دكتورة نساء؟")
- Faster response time for availability queries

### Dashboard
- New date range filter on the Appointments page
- Appointment status updates now reflected in real time without page refresh
- Improved mobile layout for the doctor schedule view

---

## July 2025

### Knowledge base
- Knowledge base documents now indexed faster after upload (under 30 seconds for most PDFs)
- Improved accuracy when the AI references uploaded PDFs in conversations

### Billing
- Overage charges are now itemized separately on invoices
- New payment retry flow for failed card transactions

### Widget
- Widget chat panel now adapts correctly to RTL (Arabic) and LTR (English) in a single session
- Reduced widget JavaScript bundle size by 40% for faster page load on clinic websites

---

## June 2025

### WhatsApp
- Improved session stability — connections now persist longer without requiring re-linking
- New "WhatsApp disconnected" notification to alert your team when the session drops

### AI agent
- Added support for multi-branch availability queries ("فين الفرع اللي فيه دكتور كذا؟")
- The AI can now handle cancellation requests more reliably even when patients give vague references to their booking

### Team
- Branch assignment for team members now works correctly for employees with multiple branch access
