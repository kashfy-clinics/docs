Doctors get their own workspace, separate from the clinic dashboard, that shows only their own work. They sign in at [app.kashfy.site](https://app.kashfy.site) like everyone else and land in the portal.

## Joining

The clinic adds the doctor on the Doctors page and sends them an invite link. The doctor opens it, continues with Google or verifies their email with a code, sets a password, and taps **Activate account**. See [Inviting the doctor](/doctors/overview#inviting-the-doctor).

## What doctors see

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="calendar-days" title="Appointments">
    Opens on today's bookings for this doctor, with the same board, calendar, and date picker the clinic uses. Doctors can move their own bookings between stages and open the visit sheet to write the diagnosis, the [prescription](/appointments/prescriptions), lab and scan orders, and chart notes. See [Managing Appointments](/appointments/managing-appointments).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="users" title="My Patients">
    Every patient who has had at least one booking with this doctor. Tap a patient for a quick look, or open the full [patient profile](/patients/patient-profile). Doctors only see their own visits with that patient, and can update medical alerts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="messages" title="Conversations">
    The WhatsApp and widget chats of the doctor's patients, useful context before a visit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What doctors can't do

- See other doctors' bookings or patients
- Create bookings (reception does this)
- See or record payments
- Change clinic settings, AI settings, billing, or the team

## Settings

Under **Settings** in the portal:

| Tab | What's there |
|-----|--------------|
| **Personal** | Photo, mobile number ([verified on WhatsApp](/getting-started/sign-in#verifying-your-mobile-number)), name in Arabic and English, specialty, bio, and change password |
| **Public Page** | The doctor's public profile page (below) |

## Public doctor page

Each doctor can have a public page at `doctors.kashfy.site/ar/<username>` to share with patients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Enable public page">
    When it's on, anyone with the link can view the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a username">
    7 to 40 characters: lowercase English letters, numbers, and hyphens. Kashfy tells you if it's available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand it">
    Upload a logo (or **Use my profile photo**) and pick a brand color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show WhatsApp number (optional)">
    Shows the doctor's verified mobile number on the page. Add the number in **Personal** first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page shows the doctor's photo, name, specialty, bio, the clinics they work at (with a **View clinic** link), and patient ratings. Copy or open the link from the same tab.

## Doctors at several clinics

A doctor can belong to more than one clinic with one account. A switcher in the sidebar changes which clinic the portal shows.

## Branch restrictions

If the clinic limited the doctor to certain branches, the portal only shows bookings and patients at those branches.
