---
title: 'Time Off'
description: "Close a doctor's day or set one-off hours without changing the weekly schedule"
---

Time off (also called overrides) changes a doctor's availability for one date, for a holiday, a conference, or a late start, without touching the weekly schedule.

## Adding time off

Open it from the **Off days** button on the doctor's row in **Dashboard → Doctors**, or from the **Time off** section when editing the doctor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Add">
    Under **Days off / one-off hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date">
    One date per entry. Add one entry per day for a longer break.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    - **Off (whole day)** - no bookings that day
    - **Custom hours** - set **From** and **To** for that day only
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the branch">
    **All branches** or one branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason (optional) and Save">
    For example "Vacation". The AI stops offering those times immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Patients already booked

If patients are already booked on that date, Kashfy lists them with their times before saving. Tap **Save anyway** to continue. Existing bookings are **not** cancelled or moved, so contact those patients from **Dashboard → Appointments**.

## What patients see

The AI skips closed dates and offers the next open ones. It doesn't explain why.

## Removing time off

Delete the entry from the same list. Those times become bookable again right away.

<Tip>
  For a day that's always closed, such as Friday, untick it in the [weekly schedule](/doctors/schedules) instead.
</Tip>
