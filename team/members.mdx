Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Team**. Owners and admins can invite and remove members. Everyone else sees the list.

## Inviting a staff member or admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Choose **Employee** (reception, manages bookings) or **Admin** (same access as the owner). Only the owner can add admins. See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose branches (optional)">
    If you have more than one branch, tap the branches this person works at. If you choose none, they see all branches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email and tap Invite">
    What happens next depends on whether they already use Kashfy (see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

| The email... | What happens |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Is new to Kashfy** | They get an invitation email and are added to your team right away. |
| **Already has a Kashfy account** | Kashfy copies an invite link to your clipboard. Send it to them. They open it, sign in with that email, and tap **Accept invite**. The link is valid for 7 days. |

<Info>
  Doctors are not invited here. Add them on the [Doctors](/doctors/overview#inviting-the-doctor) page, which creates their invite link.
</Info>

Tap **Roles explained** on the page for a quick summary of what each role can do.

## The team list

Shows each member's name, email or phone, role (**Owner**, **Admin**, **Doctor**, or **Member** for employees), and branches (**All branches** when unrestricted).

## Removing a member

Tap the remove (trash) icon next to the member and confirm. They lose access immediately. Their past work (bookings, notes, payments) stays in the system.

You can't remove the owner or yourself, and only the owner can remove an admin.

<Note>
  Roles and branch access are set when you invite. To change someone's role, remove them and invite them again with the new role.
</Note>

## Clinic owner

The owner is the account that created the organization. Ownership transfer isn't available in the dashboard. Contact [support@kashfy.site](mailto:support@kashfy.site) if you need it.
