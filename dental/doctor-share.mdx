title: 'Doctor Share'
description: "Each doctor's percentage of their work, what's paid out and what's still owed, month by month"
---

Many clinics pay doctors a percentage of their work. **Doctor share** works it out each month.

Owners and admins open it from **Doctor share** in the sidebar. Each doctor sees their own numbers under **My share** in the doctor portal.

## Setting it up

- **Percentage**: type each doctor's percentage in the table. It saves when you click away. A doctor without a percentage gets no share.
- **On collected** or **On billed**:
  - **Collected** is the money actually paid for the doctor's work: visit payments for their appointments and payments on their treatment plans.
  - **Billed** is the work done: the prices of plan lines they marked done, plus what was paid for their other visits.
- **Deduct lab cost first**: takes the doctor's [dental lab](/dental/dental-lab) costs for the month off before the percentage.

## Reading the table

For the chosen month, each doctor has:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Collected** or **Billed** | The base, depending on your setting |
| **Lab** | Their lab costs this month, faded when not deducted |
| **Share** | (Base − lab) × percentage |
| **Paid out** | What's been paid to them for this month |
| **Owed** | Share minus paid out |

Use the arrows to move between months.

## Paying a doctor

Click **Pay** next to the doctor. The amount starts at what's owed. Pick the method and confirm. The payout is recorded as an expense under **Doctor shares** in [Finance](/finance/expenses).
