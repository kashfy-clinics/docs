**Dental lab** keeps track of work your clinic sends to dental labs. It's different from lab tests the patient goes to have done, which are in the [prescription](/appointments/prescriptions).

Open it from **Dental lab** in the sidebar. Dentists see their own orders in the doctor portal.

## At a glance

- **At the labs**: orders sent or at try-in
- **Late**: past their due date and not fitted yet
- **Lab cost this month**: owners and admins only

Filter between **open** orders and **all** orders.

## Creating an order

Click **New lab order**, or use the lab button on a crown, bridge or implant line in a [treatment plan](/dental/treatment-plans), which fills in the patient, the tooth and the work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Patient and work">
    Pick the patient, then the teeth (for example `24-26`) and the work (for example a zirconia bridge).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shade">
    Pick a VITA shade (A1 to D4, BL1, BL2) or type your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lab">
    Choose the lab, or add a new one by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dates and cost">
    The sent date, and the due date, which defaults to a week later. Owners and admins can enter the cost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stages

Each order moves through **Sent** → **Try-in** → **Ready** → **Fitted**. Tap the button on the order to move it to the next stage. When it's **Ready**, Kashfy reminds reception to book the patient's fitting.

You can also cancel an order.

## Cost and Finance

The lab cost is recorded automatically as an expense under **Dental labs** in [Finance](/finance/expenses). Changing the cost updates the expense, and cancelling the order removes it. In [Doctor share](/dental/doctor-share), lab costs can be taken off before the doctor's percentage.
