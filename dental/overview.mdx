---

Kashfy has tools built for dentists. They switch on by themselves when your clinic has at least one doctor whose specialty is **Dentistry** (set on the [Doctors](/doctors/overview) page). Clinics without a dentist see no change.

## What's included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tooth chart" icon="tooth" href="/dental/tooth-chart">
    Each tooth with its roots and five surfaces. Record caries, fillings, crowns, root canals, implants and more, with a history per tooth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Treatment plans" icon="clipboard-list" href="/dental/treatment-plans">
    Tap a tooth to add a procedure at its price. Mark lines done and the chart updates itself. Send the plan to the patient on WhatsApp.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patient account" icon="wallet" href="/dental/patient-account">
    Payments on the whole plan, not one visit, with what's left and monthly installments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Procedures & prices" icon="tags" href="/dental/prices">
    Your clinic's price list, or let each dentist set their own prices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dental lab" icon="flask" href="/dental/dental-lab">
    Crowns, bridges and veneers sent to labs: shade, due date, stage and cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dental inventory" icon="boxes-stacked" href="/dental/inventory">
    Materials and supplies with low-stock alerts, taken out automatically when work is done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Doctor share" icon="hand-holding-dollar" href="/dental/doctor-share">
    Each doctor's percentage of their work, what's paid out and what's still owed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where to find it

| Where | What |
|-------|------|
| **Visit sheet** | Opening an appointment with a dentist shows the chart, the treatment plan and the patient's account, full screen. |
| **Patient profile** | A **Dental** tab with the same chart, plan and account. |
| **Sidebar** (owners, admins, reception) | **Dental lab**, **Dental inventory**, and **Doctor share** for owners and admins. |
| **Doctor portal** (dentists) | **Dental lab**, **Inventory** and **My share**. |
| **Settings** | **Dental & prices** for the clinic, **My prices** for each dentist. |

## Who can do what

| | Dentist | Owner / Admin | Reception |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Edit the chart and the plan | Yes | Yes | View only |
| Mark plan lines done | Yes | Yes | No |
| Record payments and set installments | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage inventory | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Set what each procedure uses from stock | Yes | Yes | No |
| Edit the clinic price list | No | Yes | No |
| Doctor share | Own share only | Everyone | No |

Doctors who aren't dentists don't get the chart or inventory.

<Note>
  Dentists see the app in English the first time they sign in, because dental terms are usually in English. They can switch to Arabic from the profile menu, and Kashfy remembers their choice.
</Note>
