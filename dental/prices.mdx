The procedures in treatment plans, and their prices, come from your dental price list. Kashfy starts every clinic with a ready list you can edit: exam, X-ray, composite filling, temporary filling, sealant, root canal, retreatment, post & core, zirconia and PFM crowns, E.max veneer, bridge (per unit), extraction, surgical extraction, implant, scaling and whitening.

## The clinic's price list

Go to **Settings → Dental & prices**. Owners and admins can edit it; the rest of the team can view it.

- **Price**: change it and click outside the box to save.
- **Per extra surface**: for fillings and sealants, added for each surface after the first. For example, 800 + 150 per extra surface makes an MO filling 950.
- **On/off switch**: hides a procedure from new plans without deleting it.

Changing a price doesn't change lines already on patients' plans.

### Adding a procedure

Click **New procedure** and set:

- the name in Arabic and English
- whether it's priced **per surface**, **per tooth** or for the **whole mouth**
- what it does to the chart when marked done: filling, sealant, root canal, post, crown, bridge, extraction, implant, or **Doesn't change the chart**
- the price

## Who sets prices

At the top of **Dental & prices**, choose:

- **The clinic**: everyone uses the clinic's price list.
- **Each dentist**: each dentist sets their own prices. A dentist who hasn't set a price for a procedure gets the clinic's price.

Dentists see prices in their plans either way, because they decide the plan.

## A dentist's own prices

Dentists go to **Settings → My prices** in the doctor portal. Each procedure shows the clinic's price for reference. Type your own price, or leave it empty to use the clinic's. Your prices only apply when the clinic is set to **Each dentist**.
